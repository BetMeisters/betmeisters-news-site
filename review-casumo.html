Our view in one minute

Casumo is a proper casino-first brand. It has thousands of games, the big studios, its own live tables and a UK licence with a clean record for the current licence holder. On the checks we have finished it scores well on licence, payments and game range, and its desk review score is 7.8 out of 10.

Two things hold it back. Its marketing drew an upheld ruling from the UK advertising regulator in November 2026, and its terms are not yet read, so we cannot give a final score. If you mainly want slots and live casino from a licensed UK site, Casumo is worth a look. If you want a strong sportsbook, it is not the place: betting is very much the second product here.

Who Casumo suits

  • Slots players who want a big lobby from the major studios: Evolution, Pragmatic Play, Play'n GO, NetEnt, Playtech, Red Tiger and Hacksaw all appear in published reviews.
  • Casual players who want a simple welcome offer. The current UK offer is 50% up to GBP 100 on a GBP 20 first deposit, with 10x wagering and a GBP 5 maximum bet. That is inside the UK's 10x wagering cap.
  • Players who use e-wallets. PayPal, Apple Pay, Skrill and Trustly are all offered, and reviews report e-wallet withdrawals in hours, not days.

Who should look elsewhere: anyone who wants a VIP scheme (there isn't one), phone support (there isn't any), or a serious sportsbook. We have no price data on Casumo's betting odds yet, so we cannot say whether its prices are fair.

Games: what the lobby really looks like

This is where Casumo earns its place. Published reviews count between 3,500 and 5,000+ games, which is near the top of the UK market, so we score game count at 10. The quality of studios is also top tier.

Where the reviews get muddled is the detail. Provider counts in the reviews we read range from 29 to 57+, and live-table counts from a handful to over 100. That spread usually means reviewers counted at different times or in different countries. Our rule is simple: when sources disagree, we score at the cautious end until we count for ourselves. That is why providers sit at 5 and live casino at 7.5 for now.

Jackpots are well covered, with 75+ jackpot slots reported including Mega Moolah, although that figure comes from a non-UK review and needs confirming on the UK site. Casumo-branded live blackjack and roulette tables are a nice touch. Its own live studio is unusual for a site of this size.

RTP, the number that matters most for slots. Many slots come in several versions with different returns to player, for example 96%, 94% or 92%. The game looks identical but pays back less over time. We have not yet run our 10-slot RTP check on Casumo, so that criterion is unscored. It is the single most important casino check we do, and it will move the score either way.

Payments and withdrawals

Casumo scores 8.8 on payments from the checks done so far. The method list is strong: Visa and Mastercard debit, PayPal, Apple Pay, Trustly, Skrill and bank transfer, and three published reviews agree on it.

Fees are mostly absent, but not completely. Two reviews report a GBP 3 fee on withdrawals under GBP 10. It is small, but it is a fee on your own money, so we mark it down to 7.5. Reported withdrawal times are good: e-wallets in roughly 2 to 24 hours and cards within three working days. We do not score speed until we have made our own test withdrawal, because advertised times and real times are often different things.

ID checks are the other half of getting paid. Casumo says it aims to verify accounts within 24 hours, but some app-store reviews complain that ID checks held up withdrawals. Under UK rules a licensed site must verify you before you gamble, so a well-run site should rarely need to stop a withdrawal for basic ID. That is something our test account will show.

Licence and safety

Casumo scores 10 on licence for the parts we have checked. Its UK site runs under UK Gambling Commission account 61549, held by Recro Limited, and casumo.com is listed as an active domain on that account. The regulator's register shows no regulatory actions for Recro Limited. The brand's earlier licence holder was fined GBP 6 million in 2021, but that falls outside our three-year window for the regulatory record and was a different company.

Safety is scored at 5 for now. Casumo is listed with medium customer-funds protection, which means some protection if the business failed, but not the strongest level. Two-factor login is reported in the app, and an eCOGRA logo appears in the footer, but we have not yet seen a test-lab certificate, tested login security or checked how ID checks work in practice. Those are scored when our test account is open.

Safer gambling: good tools, bad advert

The tools are fine. Deposit, spending and session limits, reality checks, time-outs and self-exclusion are all there, and as a UK licensee Casumo has to support GAMSTOP. Casumo also runs a separate site, casumocares.com, about its approach.

Then there is the advert. On 11 November 2026 the UK advertising regulator (ASA) upheld a complaint against Casumo's Spinumo promotion. It rewarded daily deposits and bets with wheel spins, and the ASA found it encouraged frequent, repetitive gambling that could lead to financial harm. We score that criterion at 2.5. That pulls safer gambling down to 7.5 overall: tools matter less if the marketing pushes the other way.

Support and apps

Support is Casumo's weakest finished area at 5. There is live chat and email (hey@casumo.com), but no phone line. Live chat opens with a bot called Umo, and one reviewer reached a person in about a minute. Published reviews disagree about the hours: two say 24/7, another says 08:00 to 01:00 CET. We will find out with our own chat test, which we score on response time and on whether the answer was right.

The apps are solid rather than special: about 4.1 on iOS and 3.9 to 4.0 on Android in published figures, which is a 7.5 under our method.
