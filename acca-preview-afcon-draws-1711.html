Four draws at 80/1: inside the Acca Meister's Tuesday AFCON acca

The Acca Meister has built a 4-fold of draws in Tuesday's AFCON qualifiers. Every leg is priced above its fair chance, but it still loses about 57 times in 58. Here is why it was built, and how to think about accas like it.

Most accas are built from favourites, because favourites feel safe. The Acca Meister works the other way round: it only uses legs where the price is above the fair chance on the sharp market. On Tuesday that meant four draws in Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers, combined into a 4-fold at 80.59 (about 79/1).

The four legs

  • Ethiopia v Mozambique, draw at 2.875. Fair chance about 38.2%.
  • Rwanda v Mali, draw at 3.25. Fair chance about 34.0%.
  • Congo Republic v Comoros, draw at 3.00. Fair chance about 36.0%.
  • Botswana v Uganda, draw at 2.875. Fair chance about 36.5%.

Each fair chance comes from Pinnacle's prices with the margin removed. Every leg was also matched by a second bookmaker or the exchange when the acca was built.

Why draws?

Draws are the least-backed result in football, so bookmakers often price them generously, especially in international qualifiers where both sides can be content with a point. Two of these legs, Ethiopia v Mozambique and Congo Republic v Comoros, are also BetMeisters singles. Rwanda v Mali is a Number Cruncher Meister Special.

The honest numbers

Multiply the four fair chances together and the acca has about a 1.7% chance of landing: roughly 1 in 58. At 80.59, that works out to an edge of around +37% on paper, because the small edge on each leg multiplies through. But edge is a long-run idea. Over one bet, the most likely result by far is that it loses. The Acca Meister stakes accas like this at 0.5 points for exactly that reason.

The calculation also assumes the four results are independent. They are four different matches in four different groups, which is as close to independent as accas get.

How to think about accas

  • Small stakes only. A 1-in-58 shot will go long runs without landing.
  • Each leg should be value on its own. If a leg is short of its fair price, it drags the whole acca down.
  • Check acca rules. Some bookmakers offer acca insurance or boosts; read the terms on the qualifying odds and the maximum refund.
  • Never chase. A losing acca is the normal result, not a near miss.
