Our view in one minute

William Hill is one of the oldest names in British betting, founded in 1934, and its sportsbook still feels built for the traditional UK punter. Racing is a strength: best odds guaranteed on every UK and Irish race from 10am, streaming from 24 courses, and a full set of acca tools. On the checks done so far its desk review score is 7.3 out of 10.

The weak point is the one most reviews skip: price. On our own price data William Hill was the second most expensive of the four big UK bookmakers we track, and its prices sat furthest below the fair price. You get the features, but you pay for them.

Who William Hill suits

  • Racing punters who value best odds guaranteed and live pictures.
  • Acca and bet builder fans: insurance, boosts and early payout are all there.
  • Casual bettors who want a big, familiar brand with strong safer-gambling tools.

Who should look elsewhere: anyone who bets regularly on football or tennis main markets and cares about price. A few points of margin on every bet add up quickly.

Odds and value: what our data shows

We compared UK bookmaker prices against the sharp Pinnacle market across 336 main-market selections on 16 November 2026. William Hill's average margin was 9.44%, compared with 8.48% at bet365, 9.00% at Ladbrokes and 10.15% at Paddy Power. Its prices averaged 12.6% below fair, the widest gap of the four.

There is a twist: William Hill still had the best UK price on 33.8% of the selections where it was compared, well above a one-in-four fair share. So it is not expensive on everything. It prices some markets aggressively and others very defensively, which is why shopping around matters. This is a one-day snapshot; the score will move as our daily price data builds to 30 days.

Markets and features

One reviewer counted 35 sports, which puts William Hill at the top of our coverage scale. In-play betting covers the main sports, and William Hill TV streams racing from 24 UK courses plus selected football, basketball, cricket and tennis. Bet builder (branded Build #YourOdds), acca insurance, acca boosts, early payout, cash out and partial cash out are all available.

Payments and withdrawals

The method list is complete for UK punters: Visa and Mastercard debit cards, PayPal, Skrill, Neteller, Paysafecard and bank transfer. Published reviews report e-wallet withdrawals within about 24 hours and cards in anything from a few hours to five working days, so we will not score speed until we have made our own test withdrawal.

Licence and safety

William Hill's online site runs under WHG (International) Limited, Gambling Commission account 39225, part of evoke plc. The licence is active for betting, casino, bingo and pool betting, and williamhill.com is listed on it. The register shows one action in the last three years: a £82,687 fine on 7 August 2025 for a failure to identify individual remote customers. The company self-reported it and co-operated. Customer funds are reported at a medium protection level.

Safer gambling and support

The tools are a strength: deposit, loss, session and wagering limits, reality checks, time-outs and self-exclusion, with GAMSTOP for UK customers. Support is available around the clock by web chat and email; reviews disagree on whether there is still a phone line, so we score support at the cautious end until we test it.
