Our method

How we score

Every operator gets a score out of 10 from the same checks, with the same fixed weights, published here. Nothing an operator pays can change a score or a ranking place. We have no affiliate deals with any operator at present; if that changes, every paid link will be labelled.

Two parts: core and main product

The score is 40% core (the checks every operator gets) and 60% main product (the sportsbook for a bookmaker, the casino for a casino). A weaker second product never lowers the score.

Sportsbooks are scored on margin, how often they have the best UK price and how far their prices sit from the sharp-market fair price, all from the price data BetMeisters collects itself, plus sports covered, in-play and streaming, bet builders and accas, racing terms and promotions. Casinos are scored on the RTP versions they actually run, game range, live tables and promotions.

Desk reviews and fully tested reviews

A desk review is scored only on what we can check without an account: the regulator's register, our own price data, and published reviews, each linked as a source. Checks that need a real account (a timed withdrawal, a live-chat test, login security) and any terms we have not yet read are left out of the score: they count neither for nor against the operator. Every desk review says this at the top and shows how much of our method has been checked.

A fully tested review adds our own test account: deposit, bets, a real withdrawal and support tests. We will update each desk review as those tests are done.

Licences and countries

We only list operators licensed by a regulator we can check, such as the UK Gambling Commission. Every review shows the licence, a link to check it on the register yourself, and the countries the operator accepts.

Keeping scores current

Each review shows the date it was last checked. Price figures move with our own data; the other parts are re-checked at least every three months, and straight away if a licence, terms or payout record changes.
