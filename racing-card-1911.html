Meister previews · Thursday 19 November

Thursday's racing: two Listed fillies' races at Lingfield, a veterans' chase at Warwick and Thurles over jumps

Four meetings and 33 races. Lingfield's All-Weather Championships card has two Listed races for fillies, Warwick stages a Class 2 veterans' handicap chase, Newcastle races under lights and Thurles has seven over jumps. Every meeting, the early market and what to watch.

Horse racingSports betting
By the BetMeisters.news desk18 November 20267 min read

Thursday's racing in Britain and Ireland is spread across four meetings. Lingfield (all-weather) and Warwick (jumps) start just after 11am UK time, Thurles runs seven races over jumps in Ireland from 12.08pm, and Newcastle takes over on its all-weather track from 3.35pm until the last at 7.40pm. That is 33 races, from 11.18am to 7.40pm UK time (6.18am to 2.40pm Eastern). The ground is good at Warwick and Thurles; both all-weather tracks are standard.

The prices below are early prices, taken on Wednesday evening. Most races are still priced by a single bookmaker, so treat them as a first view rather than a market. Only Lingfield's two Listed races are priced widely enough (seven bookmaker groups) for us to work out fair chances with the margin removed. The Irish card at Thurles had not been priced at all when we wrote this. Everything here comes from The Racing API racecards and bookmaker feed.

The feature races: Lingfield's Listed double

Lingfield's card is part of the All-Weather Championships, which began their fourteenth season this autumn, and two races carry Listed status and prize money far above anything else on Thursday.

2.03 · River Eden EBF Fillies' Stakes (Listed), 1m5f (14 runners)

Worth £39,697, the most valuable race of the day. John and Thady Gosden's Morrophore (Ryan Moore) is rated 104, with form figures 41-103; she last ran 64 days ago. Ralph Beckett's Likealot (Rossa Ryan), rated 102 with form 221-41, returns from 117 days off. Roger Varian's Little Dorrit (101) and a second Gosden runner, Yamnah (83, form 421), complete the front of the betting.

With the bookmakers' margin removed, the market makes Morrophore about 25% (a fair price of about 4.0) and Likealot about 22% (about 4.5). The best early prices, 3.5 and 4.0, are both shorter than those fair prices, so the early market here is tight rather than generous. A 14-runner Listed race over a mile and five furlongs, with the two market leaders both around 1 in 4, is far from a two-horse race.

2.38 · Fleur De Lys Fillies' Stakes (Listed), 1m (12 runners)

Worth £34,026. George Boughey's Enchanted Queen (Billy Loughnane) comes here on a winning run: her form reads 232-11 and she last ran 48 days ago. She is rated 94, well below Kon Tiki (Jane Chapple-Hyam, Ryan Moore, rated 107) and James Fanshawe's Sky Safari (106). Andrew Balding's Pacific Mission (101) ran only 19 days ago.

The market's fair chances: Enchanted Queen about 26% (fair price about 3.8), Kon Tiki about 19% (about 5.3) and Sky Safari about 15% (about 6.9). Again the best early prices (3.5, 5.0 and 6.5) are a little shorter than fair. The interesting question is ratings against form: Enchanted Queen is the improving filly, Kon Tiki and Sky Safari have the higher marks.

Lingfield: the rest of the card

11.18 · Novice stakes, 1m (9 runners)

Harry Eustace's Silk Braid (Kaiya Fraser), rated 92 with form 1-2, is around 8/13 on a first run for 208 days. The Kublers' Nascondino (78) ran 24 days ago and is next at around 5/2.

11.48 · Nursery, 7f (9 runners)

A tight race: Richard Hughes' Boy Basilica around 7/2, with Rogue Imperial (Archie Watson, rated 82, form 537211), Rosevannion (83) and Balding's Show Me Glory (form 321) all around 4/1.

12.18 and 12.53 · Restricted fillies' maidens, 7f

Owen Burrows' unraced Valley Rising heads the first division at around 2/1. In the second, Tim Easterby's Classic Bellini (form 73632, last run 14 days ago) and Balding's newcomer Star Run are joint favourites at around 9/4.

1.28 · Fillies' handicap, 6f (5 runners)

Ralph Beckett's Zooella (rated 83, form -11051) is around 15/8, with John Wainwright's Startling (form 211081) at around 3/1.

3.08 and 3.40 · The handicaps

The 3.08 is a 14-runner Class 6 race with no favourite shorter than 9/2: Marco Botti's Orange Emperor heads the betting. In the 3.40 sprint, Clive Cox's Got The Booty (Rossa Ryan) is around 2/1.

Warwick: the veterans' chase and a day of novices

Warwick has eight races over jumps on good ground. Two trainers dominate the entries: Olly Murphy, who has runners right through the card with Sean Bowen riding several of them (two of the races are named after his yard's sponsorship), and Nicky Henderson.

1.37 · Veterans' handicap chase, 3m (8 runners, Class 2)

The best race at Warwick, worth £15,609, and leg nine of the Stayers' Veterans' Chase series. Rebecca Curtis' The Boola Boss (Jamie Brace, rated 120, form 341-31) is the early favourite at around 3/1. Olly Murphy's Sure Touch (129) is around 4/1, with Jamie Snowden's Git Maker (132) and Tom Lacey's Nocte Volatus (134, form F117-4) at around 5/1. Git Maker returns from 215 days off. In a race for horses aged ten and over, fitness and the handicapper's view of old form matter as much as raw ability.

11.27 · Fillies' juvenile hurdle (4 runners)

Four runners, none with a run listed. James Owen's Saq of Gold and Harry Derham's Leslie's Pride are close in the betting at around 6/4 and 7/4.

11.57 and 12.27 · Maiden hurdles (two divisions)

The first division is a good one. Olly Murphy's L'Unique Allen (second last time), Alan King's Friendly Glance (a winner last time) and Henderson's St Gabriel (also a last-time winner) are all between 5/2 and 3/1. Paul Nicholls' Artaban is around 13/2. In the second division, Ben Pauling's Smile John Boy and Murphy's Rock On Ronnie, both third or second last time out 21 days ago, head the betting.

1.02 · Mares' novices' hurdle (10 runners)

Dan Skelton's Capricciose (form 315-3) is around 2/1. Murphy's Largy Force, rated 127 and a winner last time (form 1212-1), is close behind at around 9/4 on her first run for 189 days.

2.12, 2.47 and 3.17 · Mares' races

The 2.12 mares' handicap hurdle is open: Murphy's Easy To Follow and Fergal O'Brien's Strong Run (rated 117) at around 7/2. In the 2.47 mares' chase, Kim Bailey and Mat Nicholls' Coolanna (form 2/114-) is around 15/8 on her first run for 264 days. The card ends with a 17-runner mares' bumper headed by Skelton's newcomer Elodie Bijou.

Newcastle: the evening card

Seven races on the all-weather from 3.35pm to 7.40pm (10.35am to 2.40pm Eastern).

3.35 · Handicap, 1m2f (8 runners, Class 2)

Worth £13,190, the best race at Newcastle. The Gosdens' Water To Wine (Robert Havlin, rated 102, form 1130) is favourite at around 9/4. Tim Easterby's Stoneacre Donny (91) ran only 13 days ago and is around 11/4; Iain Jardine's Paddy The Squire is rated 104, the highest of the front four in the betting.

4.10, 5.10 and 5.40 · Novice stakes

William Haggas' unraced El Ra'ady heads the 4.10 at around 13/8. The two divisions of the EBF novice stakes over seven furlongs are both small fields: Archie Watson's High Threshold (Hollie Doyle), second on the only previous start, is around 11/8 in the first; in the second, K. R. Burke's newcomer Sede Vacante and Varian's unraced On A Boat That Day are around 7/4 and 2/1.

The handicaps

Ed Dunlop's Starmade (rated 85) is the clearest favourite of the evening at around 9/4 in the 6.40. The 6.10, 7.10 and 7.40 are 14-runner handicaps with favourites at 4/1 or bigger, the kind of races where the early price tells you little.

Thurles

Seven races over jumps on good ground, from 12.08pm to 3.30pm. No bookmaker had priced the card when we wrote this. The names to note: Willie Mullins runs Doctor Steinberg (Paul Townend) in the 12.08 beginners' chase, and Gordon Elliott has Brother Walfrid in the four-runner novice hurdle at 1.15, where Minella Emperor (Emmet Mullins) is the top-rated runner on 124 and Hi Ho Idaho (Cian Collins) is rated 121. The two William Hill Challenge Series handicaps (12.40 chase and 2.25 hurdle) have 15 and 17 runners.
