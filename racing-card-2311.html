Meister previews · Monday 23 November

Monday's racing: Kempton's Listed mares' hurdle and a Pertemps qualifier lead five meetings and 38 races

Jumps at Kempton, Bangor and Huntingdon in the afternoon, then all-weather cards at Newcastle and Wolverhampton into the evening. Every meeting, its going and the races to watch.

Horse racingSports betting
By the BetMeisters.news desk22 November 20264 min read

Monday's racing in Britain runs from 12.05pm to 8.30pm UK time (7.05am to 3.30pm Eastern) across five meetings and 38 races. Three are over jumps in the afternoon and two are evening all-weather cards. There is no Irish racing on the card we checked. Runners, going and prize money are from The Racing API on Sunday afternoon; most races had no prices up yet, so the few chances quoted below come from only one to three early bookmakers and should be treated as a rough guide.

The meetings

  • Kempton (jumps, good, good to firm in places): 7 races from 12.25pm to 3.40pm. The day's best race, the Lilies Mares' Hurdle (Listed, 2.30pm, £17,085), plus a Pertemps Network qualifier (handicap hurdle, 3m, 1.55pm).
  • Bangor-on-Dee (jumps, good to soft, soft in places): 7 races from 12.05pm to 3.17pm, opening with a four-runner beginners' chase worth £7,922.
  • Huntingdon (jumps, good, good to soft in places): 6 races from 12.45pm to 3.23pm; the feature is a beginners' chase at 1.45pm (£7,922).
  • Newcastle (all-weather, Tapeta, standard): 9 races from 3.30pm to 7.45pm, mostly Class 5 and 6 handicaps, with a fillies' novice stakes at 5.15pm the best prize.
  • Wolverhampton (all-weather, Tapeta, standard): 9 races from 4.30pm to 8.30pm, the late card.

Kempton 2.30pm · Lilies Mares' Hurdle (Listed), 2m5f, 5 runners

Dan Skelton's Take No Chances (Tristan Durrell, rated 146, ran 23 days ago) is the early favourite at about 40% on three bookmakers' prices. Jamie Snowden's Hollygrove Cha Cha (140) returns after 247 days at about 30%, and Harry Derham's Jackie Hobbs (130), also off a long break, about 18%. Nicky Henderson's La Pinsonniere and Nick Scholfield's St Irene complete the field. The two at the top of the market have the class edge; the question is how sharp the returning mares are on quick ground.

Kempton 1.55pm · Pertemps Network Handicap Hurdle, 3m½f, 6 runners

A qualifier for the Pertemps Final at the Cheltenham Festival. Alan King's Ernest Gray (126, won last time out) and Nicky Henderson's East India Express (137, second last time) head the one early price at about 27% and 23%. James Owen's Idaho Fire and Olly Murphy's pair, Road To Wembley and Ikarak, both ridden by 10lb claimers, make it competitive.

Huntingdon 1.45pm · Beginners' Chase, 2m7½f, 5 runners

No prices yet, but Nicky Henderson runs two: Crystal Island (133, three wins from his last three runs, back after 216 days) and Reckless Spending (134). Fergal O'Brien's John Barbour (130) has had a run this season, 31 days ago. A good race for the level, and the market will tell us which Henderson runner is preferred.

Bangor 12.05pm and Kempton 1.25pm

Bangor opens with four runners in a beginners' chase: Donald McCain's Count of Vendome (123, in form), Dan Skelton's Le Beau Madrik (ran nine days ago) and Ben Pauling's pair, Fiercely Proud and Starmount. At Kempton at 1.25pm, three novices meet in a limited handicap chase: Paul Nicholls's Bond Broker (about 41% early) against Dance And Glance and Cinquenta.
