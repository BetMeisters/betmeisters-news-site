Meister previews · Tuesday 17 November

Tuesday's football: Denmark v Norway for a quarter-final, Wales v Portugal and a full AFCON qualifying night

The last Nations League group games, 18 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers including Botswana v Uganda, Japan v Paraguay and Asian friendlies. Every big game with its chance on the sharp market, plus running.

FootballSports betting
By the BetMeisters.news desk16 November 20267 min read

Tuesday is the busiest international night of the month. In Europe the Nations League group stage ends, with a quarter-final place at stake in Copenhagen. In Africa, there are Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers all day, from early afternoon to late evening. In Asia, Japan play Paraguay and Australia go to Jordan.

The chances below come from Pinnacle, the sharpest bookmaker in the market, with its margin removed. They show what the market thinks, not what we think, and they are a good guide to what is likely. A chance is not a prediction: a 55% favourite fails to win almost half the time. All times are Kampala time (EAT), with UK time in brackets.

Updated 07:35 Kampala time, Tuesday. Prices re-checked at Pinnacle this morning. Most chances moved by a point or less. The bigger moves: Botswana up from 34.8% to 37.4% against Uganda, Bosnia and Herzegovina down from 52% to 48% against Romania, and Congo Republic up from 41% to 44% against Comoros. Lesotho v Gabon (Gabon 65%) and Mauritania v Burkina Faso (Burkina Faso 37%) now have markets and are added to the table. Line-ups are not out yet; they are usually confirmed about an hour before kick-off.

Nations League: the final night

Denmark v Norway · 22:45 (19:45 UK)

The game of the night. Going into the final round, UEFA's own table has Portugal 12 points and already through, Denmark 9, Norway 6 and Wales 3. Denmark go through to the quarter-finals if they avoid defeat. Norway need to win. A Danish win, combined with a Portugal defeat in Wales, would even lift Denmark to first place.

The market sees a close game: Denmark 40.9%, the draw 23.9%, Norway 35.2%. So the result Denmark need (a win or a draw) is about a 65% chance, and Norway's route through is about one in three. Goals are expected: over 2.5 goals is about 65%, and over 3.5 about 43%. Norway have to attack, and Denmark may need to answer, so that makes sense.

Wales v Portugal · 22:45 (19:45 UK)

Portugal are through. Wales are confirmed fourth and will go into the League A/B play-offs whatever happens. Portugal finish first if they avoid defeat. The market makes Portugal 67.8%, the draw 18.3% and Wales 13.9%. With little at stake for either side, team selection could change things: rotated line-ups in dead rubbers are one reason favourites can disappoint in the last round of a group.

League B: Poland v Sweden, Ireland v Kosovo and more · 22:45

Four League B games kick off at the same time. Poland v Sweden is the closest: Poland 41.2%, the draw 25.9%, Sweden 32.9%, with over 2.5 goals about 58%. Ireland v Kosovo is expected to be tight and low-scoring: Ireland 45.4%, the draw 29.8%, and under 2.5 goals about 61%. Israel v Austria has Austria as clear favourites at 54.2%. In Hungary v Northern Ireland, Hungary are about 50%, and the market makes under 2.5 goals the likeliest outcome at about 63%. Ukraine v Georgia (Ukraine 44%) and Bosnia and Herzegovina v Romania (Bosnia 48%, down from 52% overnight) complete the evening.

Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers

Eighteen AFCON qualifiers are on Tuesday's card. They fall into three time slots: 16:00 Kampala (13:00 UK), 19:00 (16:00 UK) and 22:00 (19:00 UK), with Niger v Morocco at 23:00.

The 19:00 games

Kenya v South Africa stands out. Bafana Bafana are 52.4% favourites away to Kenya, Kenya 17.8% and the draw 29.8%, and under 2.5 goals is 64%. Ivory Coast are 56.2% away to Gambia, Algeria 56.9% away to Togo, DR Congo 70.5% away to Sierra Leone, and Tunisia 42.7% away to Libya, where the draw is 32.2%.

Asia and the rest

Japan v Paraguay (16:10 Kampala): Japan are 58.1% favourites, the draw 24.1%. Goals are a coin toss at 49% for over 2.5. Some bookmakers' early goals prices for this game were far from the sharp line; that usually means a stale price that will be corrected quickly. Qatar v South Korea (17:00): Korea 65.6%. Jordan v Australia (19:00): Australia 53.8% away. Iraq v Iran (19:15): Iran 49.8%, and under 2.5 goals 62%. In the King's Cup, Thailand are 51.2% against Tajikistan, and in the SAFF Championship knockouts India v Pakistan (17:00) has India at 45.9%.

At 04:00 Kampala time on Tuesday, Canada play Guatemala in the CONCACAF Nations League (Canada 83.9%). In England, the EFL Trophy has Luton v Peterborough at 22:00 (Luton 61.8%, over 2.75 goals 66%).
