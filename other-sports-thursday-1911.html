Thursday night: six Euroleague games, Zverev v Fils in Turin, Van Gerwen v Wade and Texans v Colts

Euroleague's double-header night, the ATP Finals evening session, the Grand Slam of Darts last 16, the NFL's Thursday game and a ten-game NHL slate. Every main event with its chance on the sharp market.

By the BetMeisters.news desk19 November 20265 min read

A full Thursday night away from football. Euroleague basketball runs from 5.15pm UK, the ATP Finals evening session in Turin brings Alexander Zverev v Arthur Fils, the Grand Slam of Darts finishes its last 16 in Wolverhampton, and the NFL's Thursday night game is Houston v Indianapolis. The NHL and NBA follow overnight.

Every chance below comes from Pinnacle, the sharpest bookmaker in the market, with its margin removed, from BetMeisters' scan at 1pm UK. They show what the market thinks, not what will happen. Where we give a handicap or a total, it is the line the market prices closest to 50/50. Times are UK, with US Eastern in brackets for the late games.

Euroleague

  • Maccabi Tel Aviv v Dubai Basketball (5.15pm): Dubai are favourites away, 58.5%. Maccabi +3 is the even line and the total is about 174.5.
  • Zalgiris v Panathinaikos (6.00pm): close to a coin flip, Zalgiris 52.4% at home.
  • Bayern Munich v Baskonia (7.00pm): Bayern 72.5%, with Bayern −7.5 the even handicap.
  • Olimpia Milano v Fenerbahce (7.30pm): Fenerbahce 63.6% away; Milano +3.5 is the 50/50 line and the total about 165.5.
  • Paris Basketball v Hapoel Tel Aviv (7.45pm): the closest game of the night, Hapoel 50.9%, Paris 49.1%.
  • Valencia v Virtus Bologna (8.00pm): the biggest favourite, Valencia 81.5%, Valencia −10.5 the even line, total about 186.5.

Two of the six home sides are underdogs. Euroleague margins are tight, so most of these games sit within a few points on the handicap.

Tennis: ATP Finals, Turin

Alexander Zverev v Arthur Fils (7.30pm): the market makes Zverev 68.8% to win. The afternoon match, Ben Shelton v Rafael Jodar, was already under way at the time of our scan, so we leave it out.

Darts: Grand Slam of Darts, last 16

The evening session in Wolverhampton settles four quarter-final places. Two of the ties in our scan:

  • Luke Humphries v Tom Sykes: Humphries is a heavy favourite at 83.5%.
  • James Wade v Michael van Gerwen (closes the night): the market makes Van Gerwen the outsider, 38.8%, with Wade 61.2%. The pair met in the 2016 Grand Slam final.

NFL: Thursday Night Football

Houston Texans v Indianapolis Colts (1.15am UK, 8.15pm ET): Houston 85.9% on the money line. Indianapolis +12.5 is the even handicap and the total is about 44.

  • Pittsburgh v St Louis (midnight UK): exactly 50/50 on the money line; the total is about 6.5.
  • Washington v Buffalo (midnight UK): Washington 55.4%.
  • Tampa Bay v Edmonton (12.30am UK): Tampa Bay 56.7%.
  • Colorado v Anaheim (2.00am UK): Colorado 54.3%.

NBA

  • Minnesota v New York (1.00am UK, 8pm ET): a pure coin flip, 50/50, total about 216.5.
  • Memphis v Golden State (1.00am UK): Memphis 72.1%, Memphis −7.5 the even line, total about 228.5.

How to read these numbers

A chance is not a tip. A 72% favourite still loses about one game in four, and a coin-flip game is exactly that. The numbers are useful because they let you compare a price with the market: if a bookmaker offers more than the fair price for a side (for 50%, that is anything above 2.00), the price is better than the sharp market thinks it should be.
