ATP Finals semi-finals: Alcaraz and Zverev are through, and four players chase the last two places

Carlos Alcaraz and Alexander Zverev have both won their first two group matches in Turin. Thursday night and Friday decide who joins them in Saturday's semi-finals, and the market has clear favourites for both places.

TennisSports betting
By the BetMeisters.news desk19 November 20264 min read

The ATP Finals, the season-ending event for the year's best eight singles players, reach the end of the round-robin stage this week in Turin. Two players are already in Saturday's semi-finals: Carlos Alcaraz and Alexander Zverev, who are both 2-0. The last group matches, on Thursday night and Friday, decide who joins them. The format is simple: the top two in each group of four go through, and each group winner plays the other group's runner-up in the semi-finals.

The chances below are from Pinnacle's prices with the margin removed, taken through odds-api.io on Thursday afternoon, 19 November. Results and standings are from Tennis Majors and the ATP Tour. Times are UK (Eastern five hours earlier).

Group McEnroe: Zverev, Fils, Shelton, Jodar

Zverev beat Rafael Jodar 6-4, 6-4 and Ben Shelton 6-2, 7-6(4), so he is through. Arthur Fils and Ben Shelton are 1-1: Shelton beat Fils in three sets, and Fils came from a set down to beat Jodar. Jodar is 0-2 but not yet out.

The group finishes on Thursday: Shelton v Jodar, then Zverev v Fils at 7.30pm UK (2.30pm Eastern). Pinnacle makes Zverev 70% to beat Fils (fair price 1.43). According to Tennis Majors' qualification scenarios:

  • If Zverev beats Fils, Zverev wins the group, and Shelton takes second place if he has beaten Jodar.
  • If Fils beats Zverev, Fils usually qualifies, but he can still go out if he needs three sets and Shelton beats Jodar in straight sets.
  • Jodar qualifies only if he beats Shelton in straight sets and Zverev beats Fils in straight sets. If the three players are level on matches and sets, games won decide it.

In short, the likeliest outcome on the numbers is Zverev first and Shelton or Fils second, and Thursday's sets, not just the results, may decide which.

Group Lendl: Alcaraz, Tiafoe, Medvedev, Cobolli

Alcaraz has dropped only ten games in two matches: he beat Frances Tiafoe 6-2, 6-2 and Daniil Medvedev 6-2, 6-4. He is certain to finish first whatever happens against Flavio Cobolli on Friday at 1pm UK (8am Eastern), where Pinnacle makes him 82% (fair price 1.22). Italy's Cobolli lost to Medvedev and Tiafoe and is out.

That leaves a straight shoot-out for second place: Medvedev v Tiafoe on Friday at 7.30pm UK (2.30pm Eastern), and the winner goes through. Pinnacle makes Medvedev 67% (fair price 1.49) and Tiafoe 33% (fair price 3.06). Medvedev beat Cobolli 7-6, 6-3; Tiafoe beat him 7-6(6), 6-0.

Who plays whom on Saturday

The semi-finals are on Saturday 21 November:

  • Alcaraz (Group Lendl winner) v the Group McEnroe runner-up: most likely Shelton or Fils.
  • The Group McEnroe winner (most likely Zverev, 70% to beat Fils) v the Group Lendl runner-up: Medvedev (67%) or Tiafoe (33%).

On the market's numbers, the single most likely semi-final line-up is Alcaraz against Shelton or Fils, and Zverev against Medvedev. A Zverev v Medvedev semi-final would be a meeting of two players who have both reached Grand Slam finals and both won this title before. We will publish full previews once the line-up is set on Friday night, with fresh prices.

What the numbers say

Alcaraz has won both his matches in straight sets and is already sure of first place in his group. The bigger questions are in the other half: whether Zverev can win his group, and whether Medvedev or Tiafoe takes the last place. Both Thursday's and Friday's evening matches could change the shape of Saturday's semi-finals.
