Mexico v Jamaica: Over 4 goals · 2.30am UK (9.30pm ET Tuesday)

The Number Cruncher Meister's free tip on the CONCACAF Nations League is Over 4 goals at 3.00, published at 7am UK on Tuesday. Over 4 is a whole-goal line: five or more goals wins, exactly four returns the stake, three or fewer loses. When the tip went up, the sharp market made it about 35.7%, a fair price of 2.80. By Tuesday evening that had moved to about 38.7%, so the market has moved towards the tip.

Why goals? The market makes Mexico about 87% to win at home, with Jamaica under 4%. Over 3.5 goals is close to a coin flip at about 51%. This needs a big Mexico win, or goals at both ends.

The overnight accas

The Acca Meister has three accas whose first legs start overnight. They are accas with edge, not tips: every leg is priced above the sharp market's fair price, but long odds lose most of the time.

  • Basketball 5-fold at 45.50 (about 1 in 31): Deportivo San José −3.5 in Paraguay (11.00pm UK), Florida State −25.5 and Hindu Club +9.5 (midnight), Louisiana Tech −1.5 (12.30am) and Nevezis to beat Rytas in Lithuania on Wednesday afternoon.
  • Basketball 4-fold at 23.02 (about 1 in 18): Hindu Club +9.5, Monmouth +15.5 at Wake Forest and Rider +43.5 at Miami (all midnight UK), then Nevezis on Wednesday.
  • 8-fold at 199.73 (about 1 in 118): Troy v Cal State Northridge over 149.5 points and Dayton v Central Michigan under 144 (midnight UK), New Zealand Breakers to beat South East Melbourne (6.30am), Osaka Evessa +20.5 (10.05am), then four European games on Wednesday afternoon and evening.

The 5-fold and 4-fold share two legs, so they tend to win or lose together.

How these settle

Two of tonight's selections use lines that can return the stake. Over 4 goals is a whole-goal line, so exactly four goals is a push. In the accas, whole-number basketball handicaps such as USK Prague −2 also push on an exact two-point win, and a pushed leg simply drops out of the acca, which then pays on the remaining legs at a lower price. Racing tips like Methgal are win-only: anything but first place loses.
