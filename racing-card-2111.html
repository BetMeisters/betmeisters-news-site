Meister previews · Saturday 21 November

Saturday's racing: six meetings and 46 races, with Haydock, Ascot and Punchestown carrying the big prizes

From Lingfield at 11am to Newcastle at 7pm UK. Every meeting, its going, its best race and where the market is strongest.

Horse racingSports betting
By the BetMeisters.news desk20 November 20264 min read

Saturday's racing in Britain and Ireland runs from 11am to 7pm UK time (6am to 2pm Eastern) across six meetings and 46 races. Four are over jumps, on good ground in Britain and good to yielding at Punchestown, and two are flat racing on the all-weather.

For the big races, see our Betfair Chase day preview: the field, the market's chances and our two Free racing tips. Below is the whole day at a glance. Runners, going and prize money are from The Racing API racecards on Friday afternoon.

The meetings

  • Haydock (jumps, good): 7 races from 12.05pm to 3.35pm. The day's richest race, the Betfair Chase (Grade 1, 3pm, £112,540), plus the Grade 2 Newton Novices' Hurdle (1.15pm) and the Stayers' Red Rose handicap hurdle (2.25pm).
  • Ascot (jumps, good): 7 races from 12.20pm to 3.48pm. The Grade 2 1965 Chase (12.55pm) and Ascot Hurdle (1.30pm), the Berkshire National (2.40pm) and the Hurst Park handicap chase (3.18pm).
  • Punchestown (jumps, good to yielding): 8 races from 11.53am to 3.53pm. The Morgiana Hurdle (Grade 1, 2.10pm, €90,000) and the Grade 2 Florida Pearl Novice Chase (1.03pm).
  • Huntingdon (jumps, good): 6 races from 12.13pm to 3.10pm; the best prize is £5,413 for the mares' handicap chase at 1.23pm.
  • Lingfield (all-weather, standard): 9 races from 11am to 3.40pm, including two divisions of a Class 3 novice stakes (11.30am and 12pm).
  • Newcastle (all-weather, standard): 9 races from 2.49pm to 7pm, the evening card, mostly Class 5 and 6 handicaps.

Where the market is strongest

By Friday afternoon most of Saturday's races had 19 to 24 bookmakers pricing them, which makes the early market more reliable than usual. The exceptions are the smaller Irish races and the all-weather handicaps late in the day, where fewer firms were up and margins were higher. The exchanges usually fill in on the morning of the race, which is when our NCM Racing Meister looks for value.

The shortest-priced favourite of the day in the races we checked is Lossiemouth in the Morgiana, about 78% to win. The most open feature is the Ascot Hurdle, where three runners are between about 26% and 36%.
