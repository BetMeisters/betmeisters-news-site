Thursday night in the US: Texans v Colts, Knicks at Minnesota, a full NHL slate and Houston v Kansas

Thursday Night Football, four NBA games, 12 NHL games and 26 college basketball games. Pinnacle's chances for the main ones, with the margin removed, in UK and US Eastern times.

American footballBasketballIce hockeySports betting
By the BetMeisters.news desk19 November 20264 min read

A busy Thursday night in American sport: Thursday Night Football, four NBA games, a full NHL slate and 26 men's college basketball games. Every chance below is Pinnacle's price with the margin removed, taken through odds-api.io on Thursday evening. Times are UK first (late Thursday night and early Friday morning), then US Eastern. For basketball and the NFL the main markets are the point spread and the total, so we give those too.

NFL: Houston Texans v Indianapolis Colts (1.15am UK, 8.15pm ET)

Thursday Night Football is a one-sided game on the numbers. Pinnacle makes the Texans 86% to win (fair price 1.16) and the Colts 14%. The point spread is Texans −12.5, which the market treats as close to a coin flip, and the total is 44 points, also very close to 50-50. A 12.5-point spread is a big number in the NFL: the market expects the Texans to win, but by how much is the real question.

NBA: four games at 1am UK (8pm ET)

Minnesota Timberwolves v New York Knicks

The closest game of the night. Pinnacle has the Knicks 51% and the Timberwolves 49%, with the Knicks a 1-point favourite on the road. The total is 215.5, the lowest of the four NBA games.

Memphis Grizzlies v Golden State Warriors

Memphis are 69% at home (fair price 1.45), with a spread of Grizzlies −7 and a total of 224.5.

San Antonio Spurs v LA Clippers

The biggest favourites in the NBA tonight: the Spurs are 89% (fair price 1.12) and −15.5 on the spread. The Clippers are about 11%.

Milwaukee Bucks v Portland Trail Blazers

The number that stands out on the NBA card: Pinnacle makes the visiting Trail Blazers 80% to win in Milwaukee, with Portland −11 on the spread. Away favourites at that size are unusual, so this is a price worth checking against the team news before tip-off. The total is 230.5, the highest of the night.

NHL: 12 games from midnight UK (7pm ET)

Hockey markets are tight, and tonight's card is no exception: ten of the 12 games have no side better than 61%.

  • Carolina Hurricanes v Montreal Canadiens (midnight UK): Carolina are the night's biggest favourites at 74%.
  • Calgary Flames v Minnesota Wild (1am UK): Minnesota 69% away; Calgary 31%.
  • Seattle Kraken v Vancouver Canucks (2.40am UK): Seattle 61%.
  • Columbus at Chicago (1am UK): Columbus 60% on the road, and the total is 5.5 goals, one of only two games below 6.5.
  • Ottawa v Philadelphia (midnight UK): Ottawa 59%.
  • Tampa Bay v Edmonton (12.30am UK): Tampa Bay 57%.
  • Washington v Buffalo (midnight UK): Washington 56%.
  • Colorado v Anaheim (2am UK): Colorado 55%.
  • Vegas v Dallas (3am UK): Vegas 53%, total 5.5.
  • Toronto v Los Angeles and Winnipeg v the Islanders (both midnight UK): 52% and 53% for the home sides.
  • Pittsburgh v St. Louis (midnight UK): a dead heat, 50% each.

One betting note: some UK bookmakers' hockey totals are for regular time only (60 minutes), while Pinnacle's main line includes overtime and shootouts. Check which one you are betting before comparing prices.

College basketball: the best of 26 games

Houston v Kansas (4am UK Friday, 11pm ET) is the headline game: Houston 59%, Kansas 41%, total 150.5. Earlier, Notre Dame v Auburn (10pm UK, 5pm ET) has Auburn 59% away from home. The in-state Oregon v Oregon State game (2am UK) is close to a coin flip at 52% to 48%, as are TCU v Texas A&M and Drake v College of Charleston. Wisconsin are 65% against Seton Hall (1.30am UK).
