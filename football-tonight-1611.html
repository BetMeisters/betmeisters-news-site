Nations League tonight: Germany's must-win, Scotland in Switzerland and Greece v Serbia

The final round of the Nations League group stage, game by game, with the chances the sharp market gives each side.

Monday night brings the last round of Nations League group games. The chances below come from Pinnacle, the sharpest bookmaker in the market, with its margin removed. They are the market's view, not ours, but they are a good guide to what is likely.

Germany v Netherlands (19:45 UK, 22:45 Kampala)

The big one, in Berlin. Germany have to win; the Netherlands top the group and a draw is enough for them. The market makes it Germany 51%, the draw 22% and the Netherlands 27%. Goals are expected: over 2.5 goals is about a 68% chance. Germany at 1.87 is The Original Meister's pick tonight, shown above.

Switzerland v Scotland (19:45 UK)

Switzerland are favourites at home, about 53%, with the draw 25% and Scotland 22%. The goals market is evenly balanced: over 2.5 goals is about a coin toss at 51%. For Scotland, a point would be a decent night away from home.

Greece v Serbia (19:45 UK)

Greece are strong favourites, about 68% to win, with Serbia only 13%. A two-goal Greek win is a different matter: the market makes it about 43%, which is the line The Original Meister has taken tonight at 2.20 (Greece -1 on the three-way handicap).

Estonia v Bulgaria (17:00 UK)

A tight one in Tallinn. Estonia are slight favourites at about 50%, the draw 29% and Bulgaria 22%. The market expects a low-scoring game: under 2.5 goals is about 62%.

Lithuania v Liechtenstein and Iceland v Luxembourg (17:00 UK)

Two big favourites. Lithuania are about 89% to beat Liechtenstein and Iceland about 82% to beat Luxembourg. At those chances the interest is in the margin, not the result: Lithuania winning by three or more is about 41% on the market, and Iceland by two or more about 57%.

How to use these numbers

A chance is not a prediction. A 51% favourite loses almost half the time. What the numbers tell you is whether a price is fair: if the market makes a team 51%, a fair price is about 1.96. Anything shorter means you are paying the bookmaker's margin. Our value betting guide explains more.
