Weekend football preview: Newcastle v Arsenal, Liverpool v Man Utd and every Premier League game in numbers

The Premier League is back after the international break. Arsenal lead by five points, two of the bottom three go to top-half sides, Coventry host bottom-placed Palace, and Pinnacle's prices put a number on every game. Plus the big matches in Spain, Italy, Germany and France.

FootballSports betting
By the BetMeisters.news desk19 November 20267 min read

The Premier League returns this weekend after the November international break with Gameweek 11: seven games on Saturday 21 November, two on Sunday and Brentford v Everton on Monday night. The headline game is Newcastle v Arsenal at 5.30pm UK on Saturday (12.30pm Eastern), and Sunday brings Liverpool v Manchester United at 4.30pm UK (11.30am Eastern).

Every chance in this preview comes from Pinnacle's prices with the bookmaker's margin removed, taken through odds-api.io on Thursday afternoon, 19 November. Pinnacle is the sharpest bookmaker in the world and takes big bets from professionals, so its prices are the best public estimate of how likely each result is. Next to each chance we give the fair price (one divided by the chance) and the best price we found at the big UK bookmakers. When the best price is shorter than the fair price, the bookmakers are taking a margin on that result. This weekend that is true of every result on the Premier League card.

The table after ten games

Arsenal lead on 27 points from 10 games: nine wins and one defeat. Brighton are second on 22, with the best goal difference in the league (+19), and Manchester City are third on 21. Liverpool are fourth on 18, and Chelsea and Everton are level on 16.

At the bottom, Crystal Palace are 20th on 7 points with a goal difference of −14. Sunderland and Manchester United have 8 points each. Coventry and Nottingham Forest are just above them on 9. Only three points separate 15th-placed Fulham (10) from the bottom, so six teams are in the relegation fight. (Table after Gameweek 10, which ended on 8 November.)

The big one: Newcastle v Arsenal (Saturday 5.30pm UK)

The leaders go to St James' Park. Newcastle are 12th on 12 points, fifteen behind Arsenal, and the market makes Arsenal clear favourites: 62% to win (fair price 1.62), with the draw at 22% and a Newcastle win at 16% (fair price 6.13). The best UK price on Arsenal is 1.60, almost exactly fair, which tells you how closely the bookmakers are watching this game. Pinnacle makes three or more goals 56% likely.

A 62% favourite still fails to win almost two times in five. Arsenal have dropped only three points all season, so a Newcastle win or a draw would be the biggest shake-up of the weekend. We previewed this game in full earlier in the week.

Liverpool v Manchester United (Sunday 4.30pm UK)

Liverpool are fourth, United are 18th, and ten points separate them. Pinnacle makes Liverpool 55% (fair price 1.83), with the draw and a United win both at about 23% (fair price about 4.4). At 65%, Over 2.5 goals is the joint-highest goals expectation of the weekend, level with Manchester City v Fulham. United go into the game in the relegation zone on 8 points; a draw would take them level with Coventry and Forest on 9. Our full preview of this game is in the previews section.

The relegation six-pointer: Coventry v Crystal Palace (Saturday 3pm UK)

The tightest game on the card. Coventry (17th, 9 points) host bottom-placed Palace (7 points), and Pinnacle barely separates them: Coventry 39%, the draw 27%, Palace 35%. A Palace win would take them to 10 points and above Coventry, who would stay on 9. The best UK prices (2.45, 3.60 and 2.80) are all a little shorter than fair, so there is no generous price in a game the market finds hard to call.

The rest of Saturday

Manchester City v Fulham (12.30pm UK)

The biggest home favourites of the weekend: City are 69% (fair price 1.44) to beat Fulham, and three or more goals is 65% likely. City are third, six points behind Arsenal, so they need to keep pace before the leaders play at Newcastle later in the day. The best UK price, 1.40, is shorter than fair.

Tottenham v Ipswich (3pm UK)

Spurs (11th, 12 points) are 63% favourites (fair price 1.59) against Ipswich, who are 14th on 11. Over 2.5 goals is 60%.

Aston Villa v Sunderland (3pm UK)

Villa are 54% (fair price 1.84) at home to 19th-placed Sunderland, with the draw at 25% and a Sunderland win at 21%. Villa are tenth on 13 points.

Chelsea v Leeds (3pm UK)

Fifth against ninth, and a closer game than the table suggests: Chelsea 51% (fair price 1.96), the draw 26%, Leeds 24%. Leeds have drawn five of their ten games.

Bournemouth v Nottingham Forest (3pm UK)

Bournemouth (seventh, 15 points) are 49% (fair price 2.04) against Forest, who are 16th on 9 points and only a point above the drop zone. The draw is 26% and a Forest win 24%. Goals are close to a coin flip at 51% for three or more.

Sunday and Monday

Hull v Brighton (Sunday 2pm UK)

Second-placed Brighton are the biggest away favourites of the weekend at 65% (fair price 1.55) at Hull, who are 13th. A Hull win is 15% (fair price 6.80). Brighton's +19 goal difference is the best in the league.

Brentford v Everton (Monday 8pm UK, 3pm Eastern)

Eighth against sixth, two points apart. Brentford are 45% (fair price 2.22), the draw 27% and Everton 28%. Brentford have drawn five of their ten games, Everton four.

Around Europe

Spain: Sevilla v Real Betis (Saturday 5.30pm UK)

The Seville derby. Pinnacle makes Betis 40% and Sevilla 32%, with the draw at 28%. Elsewhere in LaLiga, Real Madrid are 84% to beat Celta Vigo on Saturday night (8pm UK), Barcelona are 81% at home to Villarreal on Sunday lunchtime (1pm UK), and Atlético Madrid are 62% at Getafe on Sunday evening (8pm UK).

Italy: Napoli v Torino (Saturday 7.45pm UK)

Napoli are 65% favourites at home to Torino. AC Milan (71% v Sassuolo), Lazio (71% v Lecce) and Como (75% v Cagliari) all play at 2pm UK on Saturday, and Juventus are 72% at home to Venezia on Sunday (11.30am UK).

Germany: Gladbach v Dortmund and Frankfurt v Hoffenheim (Saturday 2.30pm UK)

Bayern Munich start the weekend on Friday night at home to Köln (7.30pm UK) as 92% favourites, with three or more goals 86% likely. On Saturday, Dortmund are 62% at Borussia Mönchengladbach, and Frankfurt v Hoffenheim is the most open of the big games: Frankfurt 41%, Hoffenheim 35%, with three or more goals 71% likely.

France: Nice v PSG (Saturday 4.15pm UK)

Paris Saint-Germain are 67% favourites at Nice, and Pinnacle makes three or more goals 69% likely. Lille v Lyon at 7.45pm UK is closer: Lille 42%, the draw 29%, Lyon 29%.
