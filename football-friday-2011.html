Friday's football: Bayern v Köln, Athletic v Espanyol, a Yorkshire derby in League One and Al Ahli v Al Nassr

The big leagues return on Friday night. Bayern Munich are 92% to beat Köln, Barnsley host Sheffield Wednesday, and the Saudi Pro League has its biggest game of the round. Every chance is Pinnacle's, with the margin removed.

FootballSports betting
By the BetMeisters.news desk19 November 20264 min read

The international break is over and club football is back on Friday 20 November. Most of the games that matter to UK and US viewers kick off between 5.30pm and 8pm UK time (12.30pm to 3pm Eastern). Every chance below is from Pinnacle's prices with the margin removed, taken on Thursday afternoon through odds-api.io. The fair price is one divided by the chance.

Updated 10:10 UK: the morning market

Pinnacle's prices this morning (margin removed) have moved a little since Thursday. The biggest change is in Saudi Arabia: Al Nassr have eased from 49% to about 46% at Al Ahli (Al Ahli about 31%, the draw about 23%). In Serie B, Hellas Verona are now about 55% against Cesena (59% on Thursday). Porto have shortened to about 89% in the Taça de Portugal.

The rest are close to Thursday's numbers: Athletic Club about 65% against Espanyol, Troyes about 44% against Le Havre, Sheffield Wednesday about 46% at Barnsley (over 2.5 goals now about 65%), Cercle Brugge about 49%, Heracles about 85% and three or more goals in Bayern v Köln still about 86%.

We have not added team news: none of it had been confirmed by a source we trust at the time of writing.

Bundesliga: Bayern Munich v Köln (7.30pm UK)

The most one-sided game of the night. Pinnacle makes Bayern 92% to win (fair price 1.09), the draw about 6% and Köln about 3%. It also makes three or more goals 86% likely, the highest goals expectation of any big game this weekend. At these numbers the question for most bettors is not who wins but by how much.

LaLiga: Athletic Club v Espanyol (8pm UK)

Athletic are 65% favourites (fair price 1.54) at San Mamés, with the draw at 21% and Espanyol at 14%. Three or more goals is close to a coin flip at 53%.

Ligue 1: Troyes v Le Havre (7.45pm UK)

A tighter game to open the French weekend: Troyes 44%, the draw 28%, Le Havre 28%. Over 2.5 goals is 51%.

England: Barnsley v Sheffield Wednesday (8pm UK)

A South Yorkshire derby in League One under the Friday lights. Pinnacle makes the visitors favourites: Sheffield Wednesday 45%, the draw 24%, Barnsley 32%. In the National League, Boreham Wood are 46% at Southend (7.45pm), with three or more goals 71% likely.

Saudi Pro League: Al Ahli v Al Nassr (5.30pm UK)

The biggest game of the Saudi round. Al Nassr are 49% to win away at Al Ahli, the draw is 22% and Al Ahli 29%. Over 2.5 goals is 65%.

The rest of Europe

Germany: Heidenheim are 49% at Darmstadt and Arminia Bielefeld 44% at home to Magdeburg in 2. Bundesliga (both 5.30pm). Italy: Hellas Verona are 59% at home to Cesena in Serie B (7.30pm). Spain: Andorra are 52% against Leganés in LaLiga 2 (7.30pm). Netherlands: a full Eerste Divisie round at 7pm, with Heracles 85% at home to Jong PSV and three or more goals 82% likely. Belgium: Cercle Brugge are 47% against Westerlo (7.45pm). Scotland: Inverness are 60% at home to Ayr in the Championship (7.30pm). Portugal: Porto are 87% at home to Paredes in the Taça de Portugal (8.45pm).

Later on

In Argentina's Clausura play-offs, Vélez Sarsfield are 44% at home to Belgrano (11pm UK, 6pm Eastern), and in Mexico Puebla v Atlético San Luis (1am UK Saturday, 8pm Eastern Friday) is close: Puebla 38%, San Luis 35%.
