Meister previews · Wednesday 18 November

Wednesday's racing: Old Park Star's chase debut at Ffos Las, Skelton at Hexham and the London Mile at Kempton

Four meetings and 32 races. A 155-rated hurdler goes chasing in a five-runner beginners' chase, Dan Skelton has twelve runners, and Kempton's evening card includes a Class 2 London Mile qualifier. Every meeting, the early market and the angles that matter.

Wednesday's racing in Britain and Ireland is spread across four meetings: Hexham and Ffos Las over jumps from lunchtime, Dundalk on the all-weather through the afternoon, and Kempton under lights from 4.25pm. That is 32 races, from 12.33pm to 8.30pm UK time (7.33am to 3.30pm Eastern). The ground is good at both jumps tracks; Kempton's Polytrack is standard to slow and Dundalk's is standard.

The prices below are early prices, taken on Tuesday evening. At this stage most races are priced by a single bookmaker, so they are a first view, not a market, and they will move a lot by the morning. Only one race, the Ffos Las beginners' chase, is priced widely enough for us to work out fair chances (the market with the bookmakers' margin taken out). Dundalk had not been priced at all. Everything here comes from The Racing API racecards and bookmaker feed.

Updated 07:37 Kampala (04:37 UK): non-runners, going and the morning market

Going: Ffos Las and Hexham are now good, good to soft in places (GoingStick 5.8 at Hexham), a little softer than the good ground declared on Tuesday. Kempton is unchanged, Polytrack standard to slow.

Non-runners: Kalo Athena (Hexham 1.33), My Gift To You (Hexham 2.38), Winston's Warrior (Kempton 8.00) and Navy Guitar (Dundalk 4.30). Hexham's 1.33 is now 10 runners and the 2.38 is 7.

The main moves overnight (median of the bookmakers pricing each horse this morning):

  • Hexham 2.38: President Scottie has been backed from about 4/1 into about 9/4, and is now joint-favourite with Wal Buck's (also about 9/4, from 15/8).
  • Ffos Las 2.18: Wannabe is now a clear favourite at about 2/1; Walk Tall is about 3/1. On Tuesday they were joint favourites at around 7/2.
  • Kempton 6.00: Divine Rhythm has shortened from about 5/2 to about 6/4.
  • Kempton 7.00: Palladas is now about 6/5 (from 13/8) and Princling about 9/4.
  • Ffos Las 3.18: For Old Times Sake is now favourite at about 2/1, ahead of Wispit Twob (about 3/1).

Dundalk is now priced. The favourites are around 6/4 or bigger in every race except the 5.07 fillies' maiden, where Rhapsodic is about 4/6, and the 3.30 nursery, where Rock On Johnny is about evens.

The prices above are from The Racing API's racecards at about 07:37 Kampala. Prices move all morning; check before you bet.

The race of the day: Ffos Las, 1.48

Beginners' chase, 2m (5 runners, Class 3)

The best race on Wednesday on official ratings, and the only one the whole market has priced. Two smart hurdlers make their debuts over fences. Old Park Star (Nicky Henderson, Nico de Boinville) is rated 155 and has won his last four races: his form figures read /1111. Sober Glory (Philip Hobbs and Johnson White, Ben Jones) is rated 153, form 41124. Both have been off since the spring, Old Park Star for 253 days and Sober Glory for 222.

About 19 bookmakers price the race. With their margins removed, the market makes Old Park Star about 56% (fair price about 1.79) and Sober Glory about 35% (about 2.84). Alan King's Baron Noir (rated 146) is the only other horse with a realistic chance at around 12/1. The best prices from the big UK firms on Tuesday evening, 1.73 and 2.63, were both a little below those fair prices: the market is fair here, not generous.

Novice chasers are the least predictable favourites in jump racing. A horse rated 155 over hurdles has the engine, but jumping fences at speed is a new skill, and one bad mistake ends the race. The market's 56% means Old Park Star gets beaten more than four times in ten.

Hexham: Go North qualifiers on good ground

Hexham has six races over jumps and a closing bumper. The trainer to note is Lucinda Russell and Michael Scudamore: nine winners from 42 runners in the last 14 days, with four runners here. Jamie Snowden (7 from 22) has two.

12.33 · Novices' hurdle, 2m (9 runners)

Snowden's Lord du Gouet (Gavin Sheehan) is the early favourite at around 8/13. His form reads 13-2, and he ran 28 days ago. Sandy Thomson's Eagle Warrior, second last time, is next at around 7/2.

1.03 · Beginners' chase, 3m (4 runners, Class 3)

A small field but a good one. Russell and Scudamore's Derryhassen Paddy (Derek Fox) is top-rated on 142 and around evens. Snowden's Julius des Pictons (135) has not run for 593 days but won three of his five races before that; he is around 13/8. Skelton's Fishery Lane (131) completes the serious trio.

1.33 and 2.08 · Handicap hurdles, 2m4f (two divisions)

Mark Walford's De Deli Counter won 12 days ago and heads the first division at around 11/4. In the second, Susan Corbett's Ratemenow, second last time out, is the top-rated runner on 97 and around 2/1. Both divisions are Class 5 races of 10 and 11 runners, where the favourite wins far less often than in a novice race.

2.38 · Long-distance handicap chase, 3m7f (8 runners)

Almost four miles, and a qualifier for the Go North Red Rum series. Russell and Scudamore's Wal Buck's (form 2144-2) is around 15/8, with Alan King's Betterforeveryone at around 5/2. James Owen's My Gift To You is top-rated on 120. Stamina will decide it.

3.08 and 3.38 · Handicap chase and bumper

The 3.08 is a 12-runner Class 5 handicap with no clear favourite: Scairp Dubh (around 4/1) and Micky Hammond's Halpha Soleil (around 9/2, second in each of his last two) head the betting. The card closes with a seven-runner bumper. David Pipe's Max Potential and Skelton's newcomer Rio Blue are close in the betting at around 11/10 and 11/8.

Ffos Las: the rest of the card

Seven races in all. Dan Skelton is again the trainer in form, with 19 winners from 80 runners in 14 days (about 24%). He has twelve runners across Wednesday's cards.

12.48 and 1.18 · Maiden hurdles, 2m (two divisions)

In the first, David Pipe's No Walkover is around 4/6 on his return from 284 days off; his form reads 2/118. Joe Tizzard's Bob Bishop (off for 574 days) is next. Note that Pipe's yard has had no winners from 11 runners in the last 14 days. In the second, Skelton's Diamond Street (Harry Skelton) is around 10/11, with Philip Hobbs and Johnson White's Quickkick, second 27 days ago, at around 7/2.

2.18 · Novices' handicap chase, 2m7f (15 runners)

The biggest field of the day at Ffos Las. Tizzard's Walk Tall (Harry Cobden), top-rated on 100, and Tom Lacey's Wannabe are joint favourites at around 7/2. Both unseated their riders last time. Lacey has had five winners from 11 runners in two weeks.

2.48 to 3.48 · Maiden hurdle and two handicaps

In the 2.48 maiden hurdle, Ben Pauling's Merry Away is around 10/11. In the 3.18 novices' handicap hurdle, Martin Keighley's Wispit Twob, who won last time (form 0P-P81), is around 3/1 under Sean Bowen. Fergal O'Brien's Unleash The Beast heads the 3.48 handicap chase at around 7/4, top-rated on 98 but returning from 270 days off.

Kempton: the evening all-weather card

Eight races from 4.25pm to 8.30pm on Polytrack, described as standard to slow. Two of them are Class 2.

4.25 and 5.00 · Novice stakes for two-year-olds, 6f

James Fanshawe's Tredegar Square (rated 77, form 15) and Ed Walker's Brave Country (75, form 23) head the first, around 13/8 and 2/1. In the second, Marco Botti's Angel Footsteps, second on the only previous start, is around 5/6, with the Gosdens' Amandari next.

5.30 and 6.00 · Nurseries

The 5.30 has five runners; George Boughey's Divine Whisper is top-rated on 86 and around 15/8. In the 6.00 over a mile, William Haggas' Divine Rhythm (form 34) is around 5/2.

6.30 · Juddmonte EBF maiden, 1m (14 runners, Class 2)

Andrew Balding's Sterling Cooper (third last time) and the Gosdens' Lady Margo (fourth on her debut 21 days ago) head the betting at around 5/2 and 7/2. Maidens like this can produce smart horses for next season, so watch the market for any late support.

7.00 · London Mile Series qualifier, 1m handicap (14 runners, Class 2)

The best handicap of the evening. Charlie Appleby's Palladas (rated 95, form 114) is favourite at around 13/8. Haggas' Princling (90) is next at around 7/2. The top-rated runner is Ebt's Guard on 104, who has been placed in his last three races without winning, at around 6/1. In a 14-runner handicap the early favourite at 13/8 looks short, and prices will spread out as more firms price it.

7.30 to 8.30 · The late handicaps

In the 7.30 London Stayers' qualifier over two miles, Roger Varian's Zachary Hickes, who won last time (form 641231), is around 5/4. The 8.00 over seven furlongs is wide open, with Fanshawe's Enacting heading a 14-runner field at around 7/2. Haggas' Crown Of Crowns (form 41) is around 2/1 in the 8.30. Snooker McCrew is the one to watch for form: he has won four in a row.

Dundalk

Dundalk's eight races run from 2.00pm to 5.40pm, five of them with 16 or 17 runners. Bookmakers had not priced the card on Tuesday evening. On ratings, the stand-out runners include Golddream (75) in the 4.30 handicap and Stooked (73) in the 4.00 maiden. Big fields at Dundalk are among the hardest betting races of the week; each-way terms matter more than the win price.

What the early market says

The shortest early prices are Lord du Gouet, No Walkover, Angel Footsteps, Old Park Star, Diamond Street and Merry Away, all around evens or shorter. Most of them are returning from a break or making a debut over a new obstacle, and most were priced by a single firm. Single-firm prices are not a market: once more bookmakers price these races on Wednesday morning, expect several to drift.
