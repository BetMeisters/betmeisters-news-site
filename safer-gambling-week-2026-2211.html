Safer Gambling Week ends today: what the tenth edition is about, and the figures behind it

The industry's annual campaign runs from 16 to 22 November, backed by the Gambling Commission. Its organisers say deposit limits set during last year's week rose 41%. The numbers are the industry's own.

Safer gamblingUKGeneral news
By the BetMeisters.news desk22 November 20263 min read

Safer Gambling Week, the industry's yearly campaign to promote the tools and support available to people who bet, finishes today. The tenth edition ran from 16 to 22 November 2026. It is organised by the Betting and Gaming Council (BGC) together with the trade bodies Bacta and the Bingo Association, and it covers online betting as well as betting shops, bingo halls and arcades.

Backing from the regulator

The Gambling Commission's acting chief executive, Sarah Gardner, backed the campaign in a statement issued by the organisers. She welcomed efforts that keep safer gambling in the spotlight, adding that "there is always more to do".

The figures, and where they come from

The organisers have published these figures. They are the industry's own counts and have not been independently audited:

  • more than 50 million safer gambling messages since the first campaign in 2016, almost 11 million of them in 2025 (up 75% on the year before);
  • more than one million players setting a deposit limit during the campaign since 2016;
  • in the 2025 week, 281,000 deposit limits set by nearly 154,000 account holders, up 41% on 2024.

A rise in limits set during one week tells us people are using the tools. It does not on its own show whether harm went down, which is why independent data such as the Gambling Commission's own survey matters as well.

What it means for you

The tools the campaign promotes are there all year, not just this week. Every GB-licensed bookmaker has to offer deposit limits, time-outs and self-exclusion, and since 31 October 2025 new customers are asked to set a limit before their first deposit. Our guide Deposit limits, time-outs and GAMSTOP explains each one and how to set it up.

The campaign's own site is safergamblinguk.org. Free, confidential help in Great Britain: the National Gambling Helpline on 0808 8020 133.
