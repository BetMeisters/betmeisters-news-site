Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Who we are

BetMeisters.news is the sports and betting news site from BetMeisters, the sports tipping service at BetMeisters.com. Both are preparing to launch. For anything about your data, email tom@betmeisters.com.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • Your confirmation that you are 18 or over, and the email permission you choose.
  • The date and time you signed up and confirmed, and technical details our email provider records with a sign-up (such as your IP address), so we can show you gave consent.

Why we use it

Only to tell you about BetMeisters.news and BetMeisters: our launch and occasional news. We use it because you asked us to (your consent). You can withdraw your consent at any time by clicking "unsubscribe" in any email or by emailing us.

Who else handles it

  • Netlify, our website host, stores sign-ups from this page until we launch. Before we send any email we will move the list to an email service provider and update this policy.

These providers may process data outside the UK; they use the legal safeguards required for that. We never sell or share your details with anyone else, including bookmakers.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. If you unsubscribe, we keep only a record that you did, so we don't email you again.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages people read, only if you accept analytics cookies in the banner. Until you accept, Google Analytics is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set. If you accept, Google (our analytics provider) sets cookies such as _ga and _ga_* that last up to 2 years and processes your IP address (shortened) and device details on our behalf; Google may process this data outside the UK under the required safeguards. We use this only to improve the site, never for advertising. You can change your mind at any time: change cookie settings.

We also store your cookie choice in your browser (local storage) so we do not ask again.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can object to how we use it. Email tom@betmeisters.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Age

BetMeisters.news and BetMeisters are for adults only. Please do not sign up if you are under 18.
