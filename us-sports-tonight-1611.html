US sports overnight: Ravens v Chargers on Monday Night Football, eight NBA games and a big college slate

Monday night in the US runs through the Kampala small hours. The sharp market's spreads and totals for the NFL, NBA, NHL and college basketball, plus the two running.

BasketballNFLIce hockeySports betting
By the BetMeisters.news desk16 November 20265 min read

The US sports night starts as Kampala goes to bed. College basketball tips off from 2am, the NBA from 3am, the NHL from 3.30am, and Monday Night Football kicks off at 4.15am. Below is what the sharpest market makes of the main games, with the bookmaker margin removed. US betting works mostly on spreads (a points handicap) and totals (over or under a number of points), so that is what we show. A spread priced at about 50% on each side means the market thinks the line is set right.

NFL · Monday Night Football

Baltimore Ravens v Los Angeles Chargers · 04:15

The Ravens are 4.5-point favourites at home, and the market is split almost exactly down the middle on that line: 50.2% for Baltimore to cover, 49.8% for the Chargers. The total is low at 39 points, with the over at 50.7%. A total that low says the market expects defences to have the upper hand. When a spread sits on 4.5, the key numbers in American football (3 and 7, the value of a field goal and a converted touchdown) matter: a Ravens win by exactly 4 or by 3 loses on the spread, and a win by 5 or more covers.

NHL

New York Rangers v Montreal Canadiens (03:30): Montreal are favourites away from home. The puck line (a 1.5-goal handicap) has the Rangers +1.5 at about 71.5%, so the market makes a Montreal win by two goals or more about 28.5%. Over 5.5 goals is 56%. Utah Mammoth v Vancouver Canucks (05:30): Utah −1.5 is 55.3%, a big number for a puck line, and the total is set at 6.5.

College basketball

College basketball's opening weeks bring huge spreads, because big programmes schedule home games against much smaller schools. Tonight Maryland are −34.5 against Alcorn State, Minnesota −34 against Western Illinois, Ohio State −33 against Prairie View A&M, Texas A&M −33 against Southeastern Louisiana and UCLA −31.5 against Long Beach State. Even at those margins the market prices each spread close to 50/50.

The closer games: Missouri State v Abilene Christian (−5.5, exactly 50/50), Tulsa v Western Kentucky (−6.5) and Drake v Tarleton State (−9). Kent State v Eastern Kentucky has the highest college total at 162.
