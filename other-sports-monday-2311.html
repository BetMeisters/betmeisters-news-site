Monday away from football: BYU v Washington, the Basketball Africa League, Challenger tennis and Polish volleyball

A quieter Monday: college basketball's best game is BYU v Washington late, Nairobi City Thunder play Urunani in the Basketball Africa League, Challenger tennis runs all evening and Lublin are big favourites in Poland. Every main game with its chance on the sharp market.

Monday is a quieter day away from football. In our price scan, the afternoon and evening belong to the Challenger tours, college basketball and a few volleyball leagues. All times are UK; the chances are from the sharp market with the bookmaker margin removed, taken around midday on Monday. The NBA and NHL tonight are in our late US preview.

Basketball: Africa and the US colleges

  • Dar City v Ferroviario Beira (Basketball Africa League), 2pm: Dar City 92.2%, one of the shortest prices of the day.
  • Nairobi City Thunder v Urunani BC (Basketball Africa League), 5pm: Nairobi 75.8%.
  • BYU Cougars v Washington Huskies (NCAA), 10pm: BYU 87.4%, a Big 12 side against a Big Ten one.
  • Oregon State v Tulsa (NCAA), 7pm: Oregon State 71.0%.
  • Belmont v Yale (NCAA), 9pm: Belmont 59.0%; Georgia State v Tulane, 9pm, is close at 53.2% to 46.8%.

Tennis: the Challenger tour

  • Monastir, Tunisia, from 2.20pm: Dali Blanch 62.3% against Kasra Rahmani; Gabriel Debru 63.2% against Sandro Kopp at 3.30pm.
  • San Salvador, El Salvador, from 4pm: Axel Nefve 83.3% and Ofek Shimanov 84.0% are the clearest favourites; Darian King v Theodore Dean at 6.20pm is close to even, King 51.9%.
  • WTA 125, Buenos Aires: Martina Trevisan 71.3% against Ayana Akli at 2pm, Polona Hercog 61.4% against Ipek Oz at 3.10pm.

Challenger matches are lower-profile and prices can move more than on the main tour, especially when a player withdraws late. Check how your bookmaker settles a retirement before you bet.

Volleyball and esports

  • LKPS Lublin v Slepsk Suwalki (Poland, PlusLiga), 7.30pm: Lublin 91.1%.
  • Chemik Police v Stal Mielec (Poland, women), 4.30pm: Police 76.2%.
  • Minas v Sesi Bauru (Brazil, women's Superliga), 9.30pm: Minas 55.5%, the closest of the evening's big games.
  • Dota 2: Team Liquid v DO KONCA at 5pm, Liquid 72.5%.
