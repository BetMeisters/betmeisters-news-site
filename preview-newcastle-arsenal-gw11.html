Meister previews · Premier League Gameweek 11

Newcastle v Arsenal: the leaders go to St James' Park as 61% favourites

Saturday 21 November, 5.30pm UK. Arsenal top the table on 27 points from ten games; Newcastle are 12th under Matthias Jaissle and have won one of their last five in all competitions. The early sharp market, the goals lines and what to watch before kick-off.

FootballSports betting
By the BetMeisters.news desk17 November 20267 min read

The Premier League returns from the international break on Saturday, and the evening game is Newcastle United v Arsenal at St James' Park, kick-off 5.30pm UK (12.30pm ET). The leaders, who have dropped points only once in the league all season, travel to a Newcastle side in mid-table and short of form.

This is an advance preview, written four days before kick-off. Team news, injuries and prices will change after the last international games this week. We will update the numbers in our weekend football preview on Thursday. Everything below is sourced, and the prices are the sharp market's early view.

Where the two clubs stand

After ten games, Arsenal are top on 27 points, five clear of Brighton (22) and six clear of Manchester City (21). Twenty-seven points from ten games means nine wins and one defeat. They went into the break on a run of league wins, including 1-0 at Liverpool on 1 November and 1-0 at home to Hull on 7 November. In Europe they won 2-1 at Slavia Prague and drew 2-2 with Bayern Munich. Kai Havertz is their leading scorer.

Newcastle are 12th on 12 points. It is their first season under Matthias Jaissle, who was appointed head coach on 5 August after Eddie Howe left the club. Their recent run is poor: a 1-1 draw at Fulham on 7 November, before that a 1-0 home defeat by Everton, a 3-1 League Cup defeat at Everton, a 2-0 win at Crystal Palace and a 1-0 home defeat by Aston Villa. Earlier in the season they lost 4-1 at Coventry. Anthony Elanga is their leading scorer.

The recent meetings have been tight. Arsenal won both league games last season: 2-1 at St James' Park in September 2025 and 1-0 at the Emirates in April 2026.

What the market says

On Tuesday afternoon (UK), Pinnacle's prices with the margin removed gave:

  • Arsenal win: 61.0% (fair price about 1.64)
  • Draw: 22.3% (about 4.48)
  • Newcastle win: 16.7% (about 5.99)

That has not moved since Monday, when we first priced Gameweek 11. Arsenal are the second-shortest away favourites of the round, behind only Brighton at Hull (63.8%). For context, the market rated Liverpool only 54% at home to Manchester United in our Liverpool v Manchester United preview.

On the Asian handicap, the main line is Arsenal −1, priced close to even money (about 49% for Arsenal to cover). A whole-goal line works differently from a half-goal line: a two-goal Arsenal win pays, a one-goal Arsenal win returns the stake, and a draw or Newcastle win loses.

Goals

The market expects a fairly normal game for goals. Over 2.5 goals is about 56%, and the main line sits at 2.75 (about 51%). Over 3.5 is about 35%. Compare Liverpool v Manchester United, where over 2.5 is about 65%.

The team goals lines say more. The market makes it about 62% that Newcastle score at least once, and about 59% that Arsenal score two or more. So the most likely shape is an Arsenal win in which Newcastle still score: the market does not expect a clean sheet at St James' Park, even from the league leaders.

Reading the whole market

The handicap and team-goals markets give a fuller picture than the match result alone. Pinnacle's lines, with the margin removed, make it:

  • Arsenal to win by two or more: about 37% (Arsenal −1.5).
  • Arsenal to win by exactly one: about 24% (the 61% win chance minus the 37%).
  • Arsenal, draw no bet: about 79%. With the draw taken out, Arsenal win roughly four times in five.
  • Newcastle to score two or more: about 25%; Arsenal to score three or more: about 32%.
  • Newcastle +1: about 51%, the line where the market splits the game in half.

Put together, the market's central picture is a one- or two-goal Arsenal win in a game with two or three goals. The single most likely Arsenal margin is one goal, but a bigger win is only a little less likely.

Fantasy Premier League angle

For FPL managers, the team-goals lines translate straight into the numbers that matter. Arsenal are about 59% to score twice or more, which makes their attackers among the better captaincy options of the weekend, alongside the Liverpool and Manchester City players in higher-scoring games. Newcastle are about 62% to score, so the market gives Arsenal's defenders only about a 38% chance of a clean sheet, below what you might expect from the league leaders. Newcastle's defenders face a side the market makes 59% to score twice or more, which makes them hard to justify. Our full FPL weekend preview, with the deadline, goes up on Wednesday.

The fixture list around it

Both clubs have a demanding few days. Arsenal play Newcastle on Saturday evening, Borussia Dortmund away in the Champions League on Tuesday 24 November, and Manchester City at home on Sunday 29 November, where the early market makes them 46.3%. Newcastle's next league game after this is Brighton away on 29 November, where Brighton are about 61%. For Newcastle, a home game against the leaders may be the best chance of points in the next fortnight; for Arsenal, it is the first of three hard games in nine days. That is the kind of context the market prices in, but it is also the kind of context that makes team selection uncertain until Friday.

The case each way

For Arsenal: nine wins from ten, away wins at Liverpool and Slavia Prague in the last month, and a Newcastle side that has lost three of its last five matches. The market's 61% reflects the gap between the top of the table and twelfth.

For Newcastle: home advantage at one of the loudest grounds in the league, and the timing. Arsenal's players are spread across international squads this week and play Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League on 24 November, three days later. Saturday evening games after an international break can be flat for the bigger side. A draw or Newcastle win is about a 39% chance between them, which is far from nothing.

The rest of Gameweek 11

The early market's view of the other key games:

  • Liverpool v Manchester United (Sun 4.30pm): Liverpool 54.3%, United 23.0%, over 2.5 goals 64.7%.
  • Hull v Brighton (Sun 2.00pm): Brighton 63.8% away.
  • Brentford v Everton (Mon 8.00pm): Brentford 46.1%, Everton 27.9%.

And one date to note: Arsenal host Manchester City on Sunday 29 November. The early market makes Arsenal 46.3% for that one.

What to watch before Saturday

  • International fitness. Both squads have players away this week. Injuries picked up in the last internationals often move prices on Thursday and Friday. We do not report team news until it is confirmed by the clubs or reputable outlets.
  • Friday's press conferences are the first reliable guide to fitness for both sides.
  • Price movement. If Arsenal shorten below about 1.60 before team news, the market is reacting to information. A drift past 1.70 would say the same in the other direction.

The short version

Arsenal are clear favourites at about 61%, Newcastle about 17%, the draw about 22%. The market expects Newcastle to score but Arsenal to score more. With the leaders three days away from a Champions League trip to Dortmund and Newcastle short of form, the price movement between now and Saturday will tell you more than the table does.
