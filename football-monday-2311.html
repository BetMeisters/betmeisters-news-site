Brentford v Everton (8pm UK, 3pm Eastern) closes the Premier League round. Brentford are 42% at home, Everton 31% and the draw 27%: a genuinely open game, with over 2.5 goals just better than a coin flip at 52%. In Serie A, Fiorentina are 50% at Monza, with goals expected (61% for three or more). In LaLiga 2, Almería are 49% at Ceuta (7.30pm). Asia and Africa: the AFC Champions League and the Cairo derby

The AFC Champions League Elite has four afternoon games. Al Hilal are 71% at Neftchi Fergana (1.45pm), Al Ain 63% at home to Esteghlal (4pm), Al Ahli 55% at Al-Shamal (4pm) and Al-Ittihad 74% against Pakhtakor (6.15pm). In Egypt, Al Ahly v Pyramids (6pm) is as tight as it gets: 36% against 34%, with the draw 30% and only 44% for three or more goals.

Late: Brazil and the MLS play-offs

Botafogo v São Paulo (11pm UK, 6pm Eastern) has Botafogo at 43%, and Santos are 59% at home to Grêmio (midnight). In the MLS play-offs, St. Louis City v LAFC (1am UK, 8pm Eastern) has the home side at 41% and LAFC at 33%. In Argentina, Gimnasia are 45% at home to Newell's (8pm).

How to read these numbers

A chance is not a tip. A 50% favourite fails to win half the time. A price only has value when it is bigger than the fair price: for a 42% chance, anything above about 2.38.
