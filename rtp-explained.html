RTP explained: what 96% really means

Return to player is the most quoted number in online casinos and the most misunderstood. Here it is in plain numbers.

Online casino
By the BetMeisters.news desk16 November 20264 min read

RTP (return to player) is the share of all money staked on a game that is paid back as winnings over a very large number of plays. A slot with 96% RTP keeps, on average, 4p of every £1 staked. That 4% is the house edge.

What it does not mean

  • It does not mean you get £96 back from £100. In one session you can lose everything or win big; RTP only shows up over millions of spins.
  • It does not tell you how bumpy the ride is. That is volatility: a high-volatility slot pays rarely but big, a low one pays small and often, at the same RTP.

The number that matters: cost per hour

Expected loss = stake per spin × spins per hour × house edge. At £1 a spin, 500 spins an hour and a 4% edge, the average cost is about £20 an hour. At 94% RTP it is £30. Thinking in cost per hour is the most honest way to compare games.

Check the version

Many games are released in several RTP versions, and a casino can choose which one to run. A licensed casino must show the RTP in the game's information panel. Our casino reviews will record the RTP the casino actually runs, not the maximum the developer offers.

Casino games always have a house edge. Set a limit before you play, and stop when you reach it.
