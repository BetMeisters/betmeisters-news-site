Liverpool v Manchester United: fourth against eighteenth, and what the market makes of the biggest game of Gameweek 11

Sunday 22 November, Anfield, 16:30 UK. Liverpool are 4th, United in the relegation zone with one league win all season, and Mauricio Pochettino takes charge of United for the first time. The early sharp market makes Liverpool 54%, and it expects goals. A full advance preview, with the numbers, the rest of the weekend and what to watch before kick-off.

FootballSports betting
By the BetMeisters.news desk16 November 20267 min read

The Premier League returns from the international break on Saturday 21 November, and the biggest fixture of Gameweek 11 comes on Sunday afternoon: Liverpool v Manchester United at Anfield, kick-off 16:30 UK (19:30 in Kampala). It is one of English football's great rivalries. This season it is also a meeting of two clubs heading in very different directions.

This is an advance preview, written six days before kick-off. Team news, injuries and prices will change after the international games this week. We will update the numbers in our weekend football preview on Thursday and in the daily preview on the day. Everything below is sourced, and the prices are the sharp market's early view.

Where the two clubs stand

After 10 games, Liverpool are fourth on 18 points, nine behind leaders Arsenal (27). Brighton are second on 22 and Manchester City third on 21. Liverpool's last two league games show both sides of their season: a 1-0 home defeat by Arsenal at Anfield on 1 November, then an away win at Crystal Palace a week later that lifted them back into the top four.

Manchester United are 18th on 8 points, inside the relegation zone, one point behind Coventry City. Their only league win of the season was 5-2 against Ipswich on 29 August. Their last game before the break was a 4-4 draw at home to Aston Villa on 8 November. United were 4-2 down and scored twice in stoppage time, through Joshua Zirkzee and a 96th-minute Patrick Dorgu header. Before that, they lost 3-0 at home to Bournemouth, 2-0 to Chelsea, and 4-1 at Como in the Champions League. Reuters reported that some supporters unfurled banners against the ownership during the Villa game, and that manager Michael Carrick would not discuss his own future.

Since then, United have changed manager. On Thursday 12 November the club dismissed Carrick, 15 games into the two-year contract he signed in May, and confirmed Mauricio Pochettino as head coach on a two-and-a-half-year deal. Pochettino left his job as head coach of the United States men's national team to take it. Sunday at Anfield will be his first game in charge, with only the international-break week to work with the players who are not away with their countries.

What the market says

On Monday evening, Pinnacle's prices (margin removed) gave:

  • Liverpool win: 54.2% (fair price about 1.85)
  • Draw: 22.8% (about 4.39)
  • Manchester United win: 23.0% (about 4.35)

That is a clear but not overwhelming home favourite. For comparison, the market makes Manchester City 68.6% against Fulham and Tottenham 62.1% against Ipswich in the same gameweek. The market is still giving United almost a one-in-four chance of winning. That reflects the gap in quality between United's squad and their league position, and the fact that derbies are less predictable than league tables suggest.

On the Asian handicap, the main line is Liverpool −0.75, priced close to even money (about 49% for Liverpool to cover). That line is a half-and-half bet: half the stake on Liverpool −0.5 and half on −1. A one-goal Liverpool win pays half, a two-goal win pays in full, and a draw or United win loses.

Goals

The goals market is the most interesting part. Over 2.5 goals is about 64.7%, the highest of any Gameweek 11 game, just ahead of Manchester City v Fulham (64.3%). Over 3.5 goals is about 43.7%, the highest of the round. That reflects both teams' recent games. Liverpool v Arsenal was tight, but United have just conceded four at home to Villa and three at home to Bournemouth, and scored four themselves in the Villa game. A new head coach with little training time adds uncertainty in both directions.

For anyone thinking about 'both teams to score', United's defensive record and their late goals against Villa point in the same direction: the market expects goals at both ends.

The other side of the weekend

Gameweek 11 has ten games across Saturday, Sunday and Monday. The early sharp market makes these the key ones:

  • Newcastle v Arsenal (Sat 17:30 UK): the leaders travel as clear favourites, Arsenal 61.0%, Newcastle 16.7%. Arsenal have won nine of ten league games. Newcastle are 12th. We will preview this one in full on Tuesday.
  • Manchester City v Fulham (Sat 12:30): City 68.6%, over 2.5 goals 64.3%.
  • Hull v Brighton (Sun 14:00): second-placed Brighton 63.8% away at Hull.
  • Chelsea v Leeds (Sat 15:00): Chelsea 50.7%. Leeds are ninth and level on points with eighth-placed Brentford.
  • Coventry v Crystal Palace (Sat 15:00): a relegation six-pointer. Palace are bottom on 7 points and Coventry 17th on 9. The market makes it Coventry 39.5%, Palace 33.8%.
  • Brentford v Everton (Mon 20:00): Brentford 46.1%, Everton 27.9%.

The other three games are Aston Villa v Sunderland, Bournemouth v Nottingham Forest and Tottenham v Ipswich.

What to watch before Sunday

  • International fitness. Players from both squads are away with their countries this week. Injuries picked up this week often move prices on Thursday and Friday. We do not report team news until it is confirmed by the clubs or reputable outlets.
  • The managers' press conferences on Friday are the first reliable guide to fitness, and Pochettino's first pre-match press conference may give the first clues to how he will set United up.
  • Price movement. If Liverpool shorten below about 1.80 before team news, the market is reacting to information. If they drift, the same applies the other way.
  • The goals line. If the 2.5 line moves to 3.0 (the market's way of saying 'even more goals expected'), the over 2.5 price will shorten.

The short version

Liverpool are clear favourites at about 54%. The draw and a United win are almost equally likely at about 23% each. The market expects goals: nearly two chances in three of three or more. United's league position makes them look weaker than the market thinks they are, and that gap is what makes this fixture worth watching closely as the week goes on.
