The Acca Meister's acca for Wednesday runs through the day in three sports: Korean volleyball at lunchtime in Kampala, two snooker matches in the German Masters qualifiers, a volleyball handicap in Qatar and a basketball handicap in the European North Basketball League. Combined, it is a 5-fold at 23.44. Every leg was priced above its fair chance on the sharp market when it was built at 7.03am Kampala.

The five legs

  • 13:00 Kampala (10am UK) · Daejeon Bluefangs v Seoul WooriWON (Republic of Korea V League): Daejeon Bluefangs to win at 1.57. Fair chance about 66.4%.
  • 17:30 Kampala (2.30pm UK) · David Lilley v Luo Zetao (German Masters Qualifiers): David Lilley to win at 1.87. Fair chance about 58.1%.
  • 18:00 Kampala (3pm UK) · Al Arabi v Al Ahly (Qatar League): Al Arabi -1.5 at 1.83. Fair chance about 58.0%.
  • 21:00 Kampala (6pm UK) · Kapfenberg Bulls v Kavkasia BC (European North Basketball League): Kapfenberg Bulls -11.5 at 1.833. Fair chance about 59.1%.
  • 22:00 Kampala (7pm UK) · Gary Wilson v Lyu Haotian (German Masters Qualifiers): Lyu Haotian to win at 2.38. Fair chance about 46.2%.

What each leg needs

Daejeon Bluefangs to win at home to Seoul WooriWON in the Korean V-League. The favourite and the shortest leg, at about a 66% chance. Volleyball match bets are settled on the match result, whatever the set score.

David Lilley to win against Luo Zetao in the German Masters qualifiers. Pinnacle's own prices on Wednesday morning made Lilley about 58%, so 1.87 is a little above his fair price. Snooker match bets need the match to be completed; if a player withdraws, the bookmaker's own rules decide how the bet settles.

Al Arabi −1.5 sets at home to Al Ahly in Qatar. A handicap in sets: Al Arabi need to win by two sets or more (3-0 or 3-1). A 3-2 win loses this leg.

Kapfenberg Bulls −11.5 points at home to Kavkasia in the ENBL. Kapfenberg are big favourites (Pinnacle about 85% to win), and the leg needs them to win by 12 or more. Overtime counts in basketball handicaps.

Lyu Haotian to win against Gary Wilson, the last leg at 10pm Kampala (7pm UK). Lyu is the underdog, but the market makes it close: Pinnacle had him about 46%, so 2.38 is the biggest edge of the five legs.

The honest numbers

The five fair chances multiply to about 6.1%: roughly 1 in 16. At 23.44 that is a paper edge of about +43%. Edge is a long-run idea; over one bet the most likely result is that it loses, which is why the Acca Meister stakes it at 0.5 points. Only one of the five prices was matched by a second bookmaker or the exchange when the acca was built, so some may not last.

How to think about accas

  • Small stakes only. Long-odds accas go long runs without landing.
  • Each leg should be value on its own. A leg priced below its fair chance drags the whole acca down.
  • Never chase. A losing acca is the normal result, not a near miss.
