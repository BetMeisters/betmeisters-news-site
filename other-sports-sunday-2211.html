Sunday away from football: Gauff leads the USA in the Billie Jean King Cup, Elite League hockey, NFL Sunday and a late NBA slate

A full Sunday: Billie Jean King Cup ties through the afternoon, four Elite League games, Europe's top basketball leagues, Italian women's volleyball, then the NFL from 6pm and NBA and NHL late. Every main event with its chance on the sharp market.

By the BetMeisters.news desk22 November 20264 min read

Sunday away from football starts with the Billie Jean King Cup through the afternoon and evening, runs through a full European basketball and ice hockey programme, and finishes with the NFL from 6pm UK and the NBA into the early hours. All times are UK; the chances are from the sharp market with the bookmaker margin removed.

Tennis: Billie Jean King Cup

  • Diane Parry v Talia Gibson, 1.55pm: Parry 58.5%.
  • Clara Burel v Storm Hunter, 2.50pm: Burel 62.7%.
  • Coco Gauff v Anna Bondar, 7.40pm: Gauff 93.7%, the shortest price of the day.
  • Ann Li v Dalma Galfi, 8.50pm: Li 85.2%.
  • Doubles, 6.30pm: Krawczyk / Townsend v Bondar / Stollar, the US pair 75.7%.

In team tennis the order of play can change on the day, and a dead rubber may be swapped or not played. Check how your bookmaker settles a match that is not played.

Ice hockey: Elite League

  • Sheffield Steelers v Cardiff Devils, 4pm: Steelers 59.2%.
  • Belfast Giants v Nottingham Panthers, 5pm: Giants 71.0%.
  • Fife Flyers v Manchester Storm, 5pm: Storm 57.8% on the road.
  • Guildford Flames v Coventry Blaze, 6pm: close to a coin flip, Blaze 52.3%.

These are money-line chances, which include overtime and shoot-outs. A 60-minute (three-way) bet is a different market with a draw.

Basketball: Europe and the UK

  • Alba Berlin v Bayern Munich (BBL), 5pm: Bayern 70.5% in Berlin.
  • CB Canarias v FC Barcelona (ACB), 5pm: Barcelona 57.1%.
  • Real Madrid v Joventut (ACB), 6pm: Real 86.5%.
  • Derthona v Olimpia Milano (Serie A), 3pm: Milano 69.0%.
  • Varese v Reyer Venezia (Serie A), 6pm: Venezia 52.1%, almost even.
  • Manchester v London Lions (SLB), 4pm: Lions 68.7%.
  • Hapoel Haemek v Bnei Herzliya (Israel), 6.05pm: dead level at 50.4% to 49.5%.

Volleyball: Italy's women's Serie A1

The pick of the four games is Vero Volley Milano v Conegliano at 5pm, with Conegliano 64.9%. Earlier, at 4pm, Vallefoglia are 78.1% at Macerata and Bergamo 80.6% against Talmassons.
