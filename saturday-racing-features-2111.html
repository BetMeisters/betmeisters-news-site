Meister previews · Saturday 21 November

Betfair Chase day: Grey Dawning heads a seven-runner field at Haydock, Lossiemouth goes in the Morgiana and Ascot stages three big races

Two Grade 1s, three Grade 2s and three valuable handicaps across Haydock, Ascot and Punchestown. Every feature race with the field, the market's chances and what to watch.

Saturday is one of the best jumps days of the autumn. Haydock has the Betfair Chase (3pm UK, 10am Eastern), the first Grade 1 chase of the British season for staying chasers, plus a Grade 2 novices' hurdle and the Stayers' Red Rose handicap hurdle. Ascot runs the Grade 2 1965 Chase and Ascot Hurdle, the Berkshire National and the Hurst Park handicap chase. In Ireland, Punchestown has the Grade 1 Morgiana Hurdle and the Grade 2 Florida Pearl Novice Chase.

The chances below come from the bookmakers' prices on Friday afternoon: for each race we took the median price across every bookmaker pricing it (9 at Punchestown in the Florida Pearl, 19 to 24 elsewhere) and removed the margin. They show what the market thinks, not what will happen. The going on the racecards is good at Haydock and Ascot and good to yielding at Punchestown. Times are UK.

Haydock 3pm · Betfair Chase (Grade 1), 3m1f, 7 runners, £112,540

Dan Skelton's Grey Dawning (Harry Skelton, rated 166, form /1341-) is the favourite at about 44%, on his first run for 225 days. Noel Meade's Affordale Fury is the highest-rated in the field on 167 but has been off even longer (292 days) and pulled up on his last run; the market makes him about 22%. Then come Jagwar (Oliver Greenall and Josh Guerriero, Harry Cobden; about 11%), Henry de Bromhead's Gorgeous Tom and Jamie and Josh Moore's six-year-old Salver (both about 8%), Ben Pauling's Handstands (5%) and Konfusion (1%).

The race shape to watch: the two at the top of the market are both coming back from long breaks, while Gorgeous Tom (38 days) and Salver (17 days) have run this season.

Punchestown 2.10pm · Morgiana Hurdle (Grade 1), 2m1f, 4 runners, €90,000

Willie Mullins's Lossiemouth (Paul Townend, rated 162, form 11211-) dominates the market at about 78% on her return after 204 days. Barry Connell's William Munny (152), also back after 204 days, is about 15%; Blake and Ebony King are about 3% each.

Ascot 1.30pm · Ascot Hurdle (Grade 2), 2m3f, 4 runners, £56,950

A tight three-way market. Nicky Henderson's Lucky Place (Nico de Boinville, 150) is about 36%, Dan Skelton's Kateira (140, won last time out 21 days ago) about 34% and Harry Fry's Altobelli (144), off for 309 days, about 26%. Fiveonefive is the outsider at about 3%.

Ascot 12.55pm · 1965 Chase (Grade 2), 2m5f, 4 runners, £56,950

Sam Thomas's Steel Ally (152, form /1115-) is about 40% on his first run for 256 days. Blow Your Wad (Jamie and Josh Moore) is about 28%, Dan Skelton's Doyen Quest, who ran 20 days ago, about 20%, and Master Chewy about 13%.

Haydock 1.15pm · Newton Novices' Hurdle (Grade 2), 1m7f, 8 runners, £45,560

An open race. Chris Gordon's Cosmic Connection (138) is about 23%, Dan Skelton's four-year-old North Shore (139) about 21% and Jamie Snowden's Wilde's Legacy about 17%. Saint Jeannais (12%) and Kingofthefrontier (10%) both arrive on winning runs.

Punchestown 1.03pm · Florida Pearl Novice Chase (Grade 2), 3m, 7 runners, €36,000

Mullins has the joint favourites, Ballygunner Castle (Danny Mullins) and Kiss Will (Paul Townend), about 28% each. Paul Nolan's The Big Clubman is about 17%. Only nine bookmakers had priced this one on Friday, and their margin was high (118%), so treat these as rough.

The big handicaps

Haydock 2.25pm · Stayers' Red Rose Handicap Hurdle (Premier Handicap, 3m, 10 runners, £56,950): Kim Bailey and Mat Nicholls's Matty's Getaway (two wins in a row) is about 22% and Jonjo and A.J. O'Neill's Seaniecon about 20%, with Joe Tizzard's Hartington (Harry Cobden) about 12%.

Ascot 2.40pm · Berkshire National (3m5f, 9 runners, £39,022): Tizzard's Rock My Way is about 22% and Sam Thomas's 11-year-old Our Power about 19%, then Brandt (two wins in a row) about 14%.

Ascot 3.18pm · Hurst Park Handicap Chase (Premier Handicap, 2m1f, 7 runners, £56,950): Ben Pauling's Vanderpoel (Ben Jones) is a clear favourite at about 35%; the rest are between 7% and 13%.

How to read these numbers

A chance is not a tip. A 44% favourite still gets beaten more often than not, and long-absent horses are the hardest to price. If you can find a bigger price than the fair price for a horse (for 25%, anything above 4.00), the price is better than the market thinks it should be.
