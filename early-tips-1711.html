How these picks are chosen

The Number Cruncher Meister only tips when a bookmaker's price is higher than the fair price on the sharpest market, by at least its bar for that sport. Tonight that happened twice, both in basketball, and both were made free Meister Specials: the picks we put in front of everyone. A 15% edge does not mean a 15% chance of profit on one bet. It means that over hundreds of bets like it, the price pays more than the chance deserves. Any single bet can and often will lose.

The Original Meister works the other way round: the picks are made by hand, with no model, and we add the numbers afterwards so they can be judged on the same record. Portland at 2.20 is a slight negative edge on the numbers, and we show that openly.

Accas are the Acca Meister's department and are kept on a separate record from the tips, because a 1-in-29 shot and a coin-flip single are very different bets.
