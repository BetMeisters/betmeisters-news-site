Safer gambling

Gambling should be entertainment you can afford, not a way to make money or solve problems. Every bet and every casino game has a cost over time.

Simple rules

  • Set a deposit limit before you start, using the operator's own tools.
  • Decide what you can afford to lose, and stop when you reach it.
  • Never chase losses or borrow to gamble.
  • Take breaks, and do not gamble when stressed, low or after drinking.

Get help

  • UK: BeGambleAware.org · National Gambling Helpline 0808 8020 133 (24/7) · GAMSTOP self-exclusion
  • US: 1-800-GAMBLER · National Council on Problem Gambling
  • Anywhere: Gambling Therapy offers free support in many languages.

Blocking software

Tools such as Gamban and BetBlocker block gambling sites on your devices. They work alongside self-exclusion.

Under 18? Gambling is not for you, and operators we list must verify age.
