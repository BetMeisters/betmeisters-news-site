bet365 v Paddy Power v William Hill v Ladbrokes: who had the best prices today?

A one-day snapshot from BetMeisters' own price data: 336 main-market selections across football, tennis and basketball.

By the BetMeisters.news desk16 November 20264 min read

Every hour BetMeisters compares UK bookmaker prices with the sharpest market in the world, Pinnacle, after taking out its margin. Here is what one scan on Monday 16 November showed for four big UK bookmakers on main markets (match result in football, winner in tennis and basketball).

BookmakerAverage marginBest UK pricePrice v fair
bet3658.5%56% of selections-10.7%
Ladbrokes9.0%37%-12.3%
William Hill9.4%34%-12.6%
Paddy Power10.2%27%-12.1%

Margin is the bookmaker's built-in cut on a market: lower is better for you. Best UK price is how often each one had the top price of the four (several can share it). Price v fair is the average price against the fair price: every bookmaker is below fair on average, which is how they make money.

Read it with care

  • This is one day, during an international break, so most games were internationals and lower leagues, where margins are higher.
  • We compare only these four plus the exchange. Other bookmakers are checked by hand in our reviews.
  • An average hides a lot: the best price on any one bet can come from any of them. Comparing before every bet matters more than loyalty to one bookie.
